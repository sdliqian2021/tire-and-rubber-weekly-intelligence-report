### KH-151 treatment improves crumb-rubber asphalt compatibility in laboratory study

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-03-17 | Tongji University research team*

A peer-reviewed study using 25% crumb rubber by asphalt weight reported an optimum 1.6% KH-151 treatment; the abstract reported segregation softening-point difference falling from 12.7C to 1.8C, viscosity from 5.1 to 2.6 Pa.s, and -24C BBR stiffness from 298 to 221 MPa. Product development is moving toward application-specific performance and faster validation. The results quantify an interface-treatment route for higher recycled-rubber loading, but they remain binder-scale laboratory evidence rather than asphalt-mixture or field-service validation. Watch for independent performance data, size expansion or a named commercial fitment.

**Sources:** [International Journal of Transportation Science and Technology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2046043026000201)

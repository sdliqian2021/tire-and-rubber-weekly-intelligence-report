### Reduced-order tire model reaches up to 20-fold simulation speedup

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-03-31 | KU Leuven nonlinear dynamics team*

A peer-reviewed paper presented a two-step projection and hyper-reduction strategy for nonlinear rolling contact and reported a maximum 20-fold speedup for a time-domain nonlinear finite-element tire model while reproducing full-order dynamic behavior. Product development is moving toward application-specific performance and faster validation. The method could shorten high-fidelity virtual tire development loops, but validation was numerical on a Grosch wheel and one tire-on-rough-road case rather than a production engineering deployment. Watch for independent performance data, size expansion or a named commercial fitment.

**Sources:** [Journal of Sound and Vibration](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022460X26000106)

### Full-scale tests validate a hydroplaning simulation under realistic pavement conditions

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-04-01 | University of Science and Technology Beijing*

Researchers combined a coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian tire-water-pavement model with a 200 m full-scale accelerated-loading track using a 12.00R20 tire, varying speed, load, water depth and lateral position. The field responses supported the model and showed that speed and water depth reduce pavement support while greater load lowers the water-lift share. Product development is moving toward application-specific performance and faster validation. The work supplies field-backed evidence for wet-road design and safety assessment, although it studies one truck-tire geometry and controlled pavement conditions rather than a broad commercial tire population. Watch for independent performance data, size expansion or a named commercial fitment.

**Sources:** [Applied Sciences](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/7/3433)

### Soil experiments show 6PPD-quinone remains concentrated near the surface

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-03-31 | Korea research team*

Peer-reviewed tests in three contrasting soils found more than 90% of radiolabeled 6PPD-quinone retained in the top 6 cm after artificial rainfall and less than 10% desorbed after 48 hours; sandy soil photodegraded it fastest while organic- and clay-rich soils retained it longer. Material security, certification and circularity are becoming commercial as well as technical constraints. The findings narrow environmental-fate assumptions for a tire additive transformation product and point to surface-soil exposure and runoff as priorities, but controlled indoor tests do not reproduce full field variability. Watch for certification scope, contracted volumes, regulatory decisions or measured material performance.

**Sources:** [Applied Biological Chemistry](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13765-025-01064-z)

A preprint combined variational mode decomposition, BiLSTM and Transformer encoders for daily RSS3 FOB price-change forecasting.

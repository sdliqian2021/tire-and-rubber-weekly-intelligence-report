## Materials & Supply Chain

### European Commission proposes excluding retreaded tires from EUDR product scope

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-05 | European Commission*

**What changed:** The Commission proposed removing retreaded tires from the EU Deforestation Regulation's product scope as part of simplification ahead of planned application in December 2026.

**Why it matters:** An exclusion could reduce administrative friction for retreading, but it is not effective law at the proposal stage.

**Intelligence signal:** Regulators are distinguishing circular products from new rubber-intensive goods when calibrating traceability burdens.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/eu-moves-to-exclude-retreads-from-eudr-scope)

### China's expanded zero-tariff treatment for Africa does not broadly unlock cheaper rubber

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-05 | China Rubber Industry Association*

**What changed:** China expanded zero-tariff treatment to 20 additional African partner countries, but CRIA noted that the change covers a specified product list rather than all goods and therefore does not create a broad new source of low-cost rubber.

**Why it matters:** The clarification prevents an overstatement of the policy's near-term effect on natural-rubber sourcing costs.

**Intelligence signal:** Headline tariff liberalization can have limited tire-material impact when product coverage and producing-country capacity are narrow.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/chinas-tyre-makers-gain-little-from-africa-tariff-changes)

### China's March tire exports fall, while first-quarter volume remains above 2025

*Published 2026-05-08 | China tire export market*

**What changed:** China exported 800,000 tonnes of rubber tires in March, down 6.9% year on year, with value down 11.3% to RMB13.475 billion; cumulative first-quarter volume remained higher than a year earlier.

**Intelligence signal:** Export volume and value are diverging, signaling pricing or mix pressure even while cumulative shipments grow.

### China commercial-tire demand improves while cost pass-through stays weak

*Published 2026-05-07 | China commercial tire market*

**What changed:** An industry review described a weakly stable first-half market: raw-material costs remained elevated, channel inventory constrained replenishment and first-quarter commercial-vehicle sales reached 1.114 million units, up 6% year on year.

**Why it matters:** The combination explains why announced tire increases may not translate quickly into channel prices.

**Intelligence signal:** Volume recovery and pricing power are diverging, leaving manufacturers to balance utilization, inventory and margin.

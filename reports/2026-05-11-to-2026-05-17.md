### Aeolus produces China's first reported 60/80R57 giant radial OTR tire

*Published 2026-05-13 | Aeolus Tyre*

**What changed:** Aeolus rolled out a 60/80R57 radial tire for 50-ton loaders, describing it as the first domestically produced tire in the size and linking it to a RMB1.464 billion expansion designed for about 20,000 giant OTR tires a year.

**Why it matters:** A physical first article is stronger evidence than a project announcement, while design capacity and performance remain to be qualified.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese OTR programs are moving into larger, higher-load segments previously concentrated among global specialists.

### State-of-knowledge review finds tire-wear health evidence remains inconclusive

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-evidence story-evidence--context" title="Reader-visible context with limited evidence">Context / limited evidence</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-12 | Tire Industry Project research consortium*

**What changed:** A three-part literature review concluded that inconsistent sampling and analytical methods prevent robust quantification of human-health impacts attributable to tire-wear emissions.

**Why it matters:** The conclusion is decision-relevant, but the weekly source is a summary rather than the full papers and therefore remains context.

**Evidence note:** The accessible weekly article summarizes the review; the underlying papers should be used for method-level claims.

**Intelligence signal:** Measurement harmonization remains a prerequisite for credible health-risk comparison and regulation.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/state-of-knowledge-research-on-tyre-wear-emissions-finds-human-health-impacts-inconclusive)

### UK moves 3.5-4.25 tonne electric vans to three-year first MOT cycle

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--marketing">Marketing &amp; Distribution</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-13 | UK Government*

**What changed:** From 1 June, new electric vans between 3.5 and 4.25 tonnes will use Class 7 MOT rules and receive their first test after three years rather than one.

**Why it matters:** The change can affect fleet maintenance timing and inspection touchpoints, including tire-service opportunities.

**Intelligence signal:** Regulation is reducing a service burden created by the extra weight of electric commercial vehicles.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/e-vans-move-to-third-year-mots)

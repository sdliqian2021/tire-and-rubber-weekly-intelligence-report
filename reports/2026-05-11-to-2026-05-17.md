## R&D & Product Development

### Aeolus produces China's first reported 60/80R57 giant radial OTR tire

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-13 | Aeolus Tyre*

Aeolus rolled out a 60/80R57 radial tire for 50-ton loaders, describing it as the first domestically produced tire in the size and linking it to a RMB1.464 billion expansion designed for about 20,000 giant OTR tires a year. Chinese OTR programs are moving into larger, higher-load segments previously concentrated among global specialists. A physical first article is stronger evidence than a project announcement, while design capacity and performance remain to be qualified. Watch field trials, mine or port customers, certification and repeat production.

**Sources:** [TireChina](https://www.tirechina.net/832461.html)

### Yokohama opens R&D center at its new Hangzhou passenger-tire plant

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-11 | Yokohama Rubber*

Yokohama Rubber opened a development center at its Hangzhou plant to localize product development and support production for Chinese demand; the associated passenger-tire plant began production in November 2025. Global manufacturers are co-locating local R&D with Chinese production to shorten product-response cycles. The operating center is a concrete localization step rather than a future research commitment. Watch locally developed product launches, staffing, test capability and China OE wins.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/yokohama-rubber-establishes-china-rd-centre)

### State-of-knowledge review finds tire-wear health evidence remains inconclusive

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-evidence story-evidence--context" title="Reader-visible context with limited evidence">Context / limited evidence</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-12 | Tire Industry Project research consortium*

A three-part literature review concluded that inconsistent sampling and analytical methods prevent robust quantification of human-health impacts attributable to tire-wear emissions. Measurement harmonization remains a prerequisite for credible health-risk comparison and regulation. The conclusion is decision-relevant, but the weekly source is a summary rather than the full papers and therefore remains context. The accessible weekly article summarizes the review; the underlying papers should be used for method-level claims. Review the underlying papers, study inclusion criteria and emerging standardized methods.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/state-of-knowledge-research-on-tyre-wear-emissions-finds-human-health-impacts-inconclusive)

### UK moves 3.5-4.25 tonne electric vans to three-year first MOT cycle

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--marketing">Marketing &amp; Distribution</span></p>

*Published 2026-05-13 | UK Government*

From 1 June, new electric vans between 3.5 and 4.25 tonnes will use Class 7 MOT rules and receive their first test after three years rather than one. Regulation is reducing a service burden created by the extra weight of electric commercial vehicles. The change can affect fleet maintenance timing and inspection touchpoints, including tire-service opportunities. Watch implementation guidance, failure rates and fleet inspection practices outside the statutory MOT.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/05/e-vans-move-to-third-year-mots)

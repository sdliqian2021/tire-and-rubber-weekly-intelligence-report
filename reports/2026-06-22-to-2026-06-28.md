### CT-derived tire model improves validation of tire-soil simulation inputs

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-22 | Applied Sciences research team*

**What changed:** A study built a finite-element tire from CT-derived geometry and a Yeoh visco-hyperelastic material model, then compared validation metrics and two soil-interaction approaches for sandy loam across 5% to 40% slip.

**Intelligence signal:** The paper links higher-fidelity tire geometry and constitutive modeling with systematic sensitivity analysis for deformable-soil traction. Reported CORA validation scores reached 0.989 for radial deflection, 0.999 for loaded radius, 0.947 for footprint and 0.985 for contact pressure.

**Why it matters:** More defensible digital tire-soil models can reduce physical iteration for agricultural and off-road tires, but the results remain tied to the tested tire, soil model and conditions.

**Sources:** [Applied Sciences (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/12/6278)

### Researchers demonstrate a vehicle-mounted route for collecting tire-road wear particles near the source

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | Atmosphere journal research team*

**What changed:** An open-access study presented an autonomous electric-vehicle platform for controlled near-field recovery and characterization of tire-road wear particles, including optical-scattering measurements.

**Intelligence signal:** Mounting collection and measurement on the vehicle creates a controlled bridge between laboratory abrasion work and exposure-oriented field research. The paper demonstrates an experimental approach, not a validated estimate of real-world fleet emissions.

**Why it matters:** Better source-resolved samples can support material screening, particle characterization and future regulatory test methods without relying only on distant roadside measurements.

**Sources:** [Atmosphere (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4433/17/7/625)

### Qingdao forum moves tire-dynamics AI from isolated models toward full-condition data fusion

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-25 | GTTF participants; Jilin University; Sailun Group*

**What changed:** At the Global Tire Technology Forum in Qingdao, Jilin University researcher Lu Dang proposed a vertical tire-dynamics AI model combining UniTire, measured data and simulation data across operating conditions. Sailun separately described a tiered AI system spanning formulation prediction, visual quality inspection and lifecycle monitoring.

**Intelligence signal:** The useful shift is from single-point AI tools toward connected model, test and production data layers. The forum evidence shows the architecture being specified and discussed; it does not establish validated production performance.

**Why it matters:** This matters because simulation-to-test data continuity can shorten calibration cycles and make tire models reusable across design, manufacturing and in-service monitoring.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2069949453033377793)

### ADAC test exposes a wide safety spread in small-car all-season tires

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | ADAC and 16 tire brands*

**What changed:** ADAC tested 16 all-season tires in size 185/65 R15. Continental AllSeasonContact 2 was the only model rated good at 2.3; Pirelli Cinturato All Season SF 3 and Michelin CrossClimate 2 scored 2.6, while eight models received no purchase recommendation.

**Intelligence signal:** The test makes balanced, multi-season performance the differentiator rather than the 3PMSF marking alone. It also shows that older-generation products still sold in small-car sizes can diverge sharply from newer portfolios.

**Why it matters:** Independent evidence across dry, wet, winter and environmental criteria can change product positioning and replacement demand more credibly than maker claims.

**Sources:** [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/ausstattung-technik-zubehoer/reifen/reifentest/ganzjahresreifen/185-65-r15-2026)

### ANRPC raises the tension between slower rubber output and firmer consumption

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-24 | Association of Natural Rubber Producing Countries*

**What changed:** ANRPC's May statistical report forecast 2026 natural-rubber output at 15.337 million tonnes, up 2.4%, and consumption at 15.550 million tonnes, up 1.3%. May output was reported at 997,000 tonnes, down 4.7% year on year, while consumption was 1.310 million tonnes, up 4.6%.

**Intelligence signal:** The monthly data point to a narrow annual supply-demand balance alongside stronger May physical prices: ANRPC reported month-on-month gains of 7.37% for SMR20, 9.56% for STR20, 6.25% for RSS3, 7.17% for RSS4 and 1.88% for latex.

**Why it matters:** Tire makers face both volume and price risk when weather-sensitive output weakens while large consuming markets continue drawing material. China was material at 617,000 tonnes of May consumption, although its imports fell 12.88% month on month.

**Sources:** [ANRPC](https://www.anrpc.org/newsla/anrpc-releases-monthly-nr-statistical-report%2C-may-2026)

### Liberia bans exports of unprocessed natural rubber to force more domestic processing

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-26 | Government of Liberia*

**What changed:** President Joseph Boakai issued an executive order prohibiting exports of unprocessed natural rubber, including latex, coagulum, cup lump, tree lace and rubber scrap, for an indefinite period. Implementing regulations were due within 30 days.

**Intelligence signal:** The policy moves value-chain intervention upstream: export access is conditioned on local processing rather than only farm production. The legal action is immediate, while the scale of new processing investment and farmer-market effects remain unproven.

**Why it matters:** Liberia is an important producing market, so the order can redirect trade flows, processor utilization and procurement risk for international buyers.

**Sources:** [Liberia Ministry of Information](https://www.micat.gov.lr/office/news/newsroom/press-release/president-boakai-issues-executive-order-banning-export)

### Chinese industry group plans Laos rubber due diligence around direct sourcing and compliance

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | China Rubber Industry Association*

**What changed:** CRIA announced a July 27-August 2 research mission to Laos for 15-20 participants, covering plantations, processors and logistics. The agenda includes quality, direct purchasing, custom processing, joint investment, EUDR readiness and transport routes.

**Intelligence signal:** The planned mission treats cross-border sourcing as a combined commercial, traceability and compliance design problem rather than a simple spot purchase. Laos had zero-tariff access to China for natural rubber from December 1, 2024, and CRIA estimated 2025 output near 600,000 tonnes.

**Why it matters:** For Chinese buyers, Laos offers supply diversification, but the economics depend on quality consistency, chain-of-custody evidence and logistics. The announcement is a due-diligence plan, not a purchase agreement.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association Rubber Materials Committee](https://rmd.cria.org.cn/a/2069247058573889538)

### Review maps controlled routes from natural rubber to functional liquid oligomers

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-22 | Polymer Bulletin review authors*

**What changed:** A new open-access review compared mechanical, thermal, oxidative and photochemical routes for converting high-molecular-weight natural rubber into liquid natural rubber and telechelic derivatives, generally below 50,000 g/mol.

**Intelligence signal:** Controlled chain scission can create hydroxyl-, carbonyl- and other functional end groups for coatings, blends, fillers, adhesives, foams and processing aids. The review highlights greener oxidants and photocatalytic approaches but also documents reaction-control, color, toxicity and purification limitations.

**Why it matters:** This broadens natural rubber from bulk elastomer feedstock toward functional bio-based intermediates, while making process selectivity and cleanup the industrialization bottlenecks.

**Sources:** [Polymer Bulletin (Springer Nature)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00289-026-06549-5)

### Linglong's Yunnan rubber story brings plantation traceability into the weekly sourcing debate

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-24 | Linglong Tire*

**What changed:** Tyre News reported on June 24 that Linglong's Yunnan sustainable-natural-rubber project was moving into a stronger demonstration phase through an ecological collection station in Xishuangbanna. According to the company claims carried by the report, the programme supports about 2,000 farmers and 10,000 hectares and combines smart weighing, data collection, farmer payments, wastewater treatment and forest-to-factory traceability.

**Intelligence signal:** The model integrates physical collection, smallholder interaction, environmental controls and digital chain-of-custody at the first aggregation point rather than adding traceability only at the factory gate.

**Why it matters:** Natural-rubber due diligence is difficult because supply is fragmented across smallholders. A functioning collection-station model could improve sourcing evidence for tire customers and regulators, but coverage figures, certification progress and outcomes remain company-origin claims.

**Sources:** [Tyre News](https://www.tyrenews.co.uk/news/linglong-builds-traceability-model-for-yunnan-rubber)

### Weekly recycling coverage returns Bolder's Antwerp rCB project to view after final permitting

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

### Michelin will consolidate most BFGoodrich U.S. output at Fort Wayne and wind down Tuscaloosa

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--manufacturing">Manufacturing</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-25 | Michelin North America; BFGoodrich*

**What changed:** Michelin announced that nearly all BFGoodrich production will move to Fort Wayne, Indiana. Tire production and rubber mixing at Tuscaloosa, Alabama, are scheduled to wind down in phases from early 2027 through year-end 2028, affecting about 1,200 employees.

### In-week reporting brings Haohua's Qingdao R&D-center plan into focus

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--manufacturing">Manufacturing</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-25 | Shandong Haohua Technology; Haohua Tire*

**What changed:** A June 25 report by Tire News via Sina Finance said Qingdao's Laoshan district had posted pre-publicity on June 22 for the LS0603054 plot plan. It reported that Shandong Haohua Technology plans a 47,800-square-meter R&D center on about 15,600 square meters of land, including R&D and laboratory buildings.

### CT-derived tire model improves validation of tire-soil simulation inputs

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-22 | Applied Sciences research team*

A study built a finite-element tire from CT-derived geometry and a Yeoh visco-hyperelastic material model, then compared validation metrics and two soil-interaction approaches for sandy loam across 5% to 40% slip. The paper links higher-fidelity tire geometry and constitutive modeling with systematic sensitivity analysis for deformable-soil traction. Reported CORA validation scores reached 0.989 for radial deflection, 0.999 for loaded radius, 0.947 for footprint and 0.985 for contact pressure. More defensible digital tire-soil models can reduce physical iteration for agricultural and off-road tires, but the results remain tied to the tested tire, soil model and conditions.

**Sources:** [Applied Sciences (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/12/6278)

### Researchers demonstrate a vehicle-mounted route for collecting tire-road wear particles near the source

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | Atmosphere journal research team*

An open-access study presented an autonomous electric-vehicle platform for controlled near-field recovery and characterization of tire-road wear particles, including optical-scattering measurements. Mounting collection and measurement on the vehicle creates a controlled bridge between laboratory abrasion work and exposure-oriented field research. The paper demonstrates an experimental approach, not a validated estimate of real-world fleet emissions. Better source-resolved samples can support material screening, particle characterization and future regulatory test methods without relying only on distant roadside measurements.

**Sources:** [Atmosphere (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4433/17/7/625)

### Qingdao forum moves tire-dynamics AI from isolated models toward full-condition data fusion

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-25 | GTTF participants; Jilin University; Sailun Group*

At the Global Tire Technology Forum in Qingdao, Jilin University researcher Lu Dang proposed a vertical tire-dynamics AI model combining UniTire, measured data and simulation data across operating conditions. Sailun separately described a tiered AI system spanning formulation prediction, visual quality inspection and lifecycle monitoring. The useful shift is from single-point AI tools toward connected model, test and production data layers. The forum evidence shows the architecture being specified and discussed; it does not establish validated production performance. This matters because simulation-to-test data continuity can shorten calibration cycles and make tire models reusable across design, manufacturing and in-service monitoring. Watch for a published validation dataset, named production deployment or quantified cycle-time result from the participants.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2069949453033377793)

### ADAC test exposes a wide safety spread in small-car all-season tires

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | ADAC and 16 tire brands*

ADAC tested 16 all-season tires in size 185/65 R15. Continental AllSeasonContact 2 was the only model rated good at 2.3; Pirelli Cinturato All Season SF 3 and Michelin CrossClimate 2 scored 2.6, while eight models received no purchase recommendation. The test makes balanced, multi-season performance the differentiator rather than the 3PMSF marking alone. It also shows that older-generation products still sold in small-car sizes can diverge sharply from newer portfolios. Independent evidence across dry, wet, winter and environmental criteria can change product positioning and replacement demand more credibly than maker claims. Watch whether manufacturers replace the lowest-rated small-size products or publish corrective evidence in the same dimension.

**Sources:** [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/ausstattung-technik-zubehoer/reifen/reifentest/ganzjahresreifen/185-65-r15-2026)

### ANRPC raises the tension between slower rubber output and firmer consumption

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-24 | Association of Natural Rubber Producing Countries*

ANRPC's May statistical report forecast 2026 natural-rubber output at 15.337 million tonnes, up 2.4%, and consumption at 15.550 million tonnes, up 1.3%. May output was reported at 997,000 tonnes, down 4.7% year on year, while consumption was 1.310 million tonnes, up 4.6%. The monthly data point to a narrow annual supply-demand balance alongside stronger May physical prices: ANRPC reported month-on-month gains of 7.37% for SMR20, 9.56% for STR20, 6.25% for RSS3, 7.17% for RSS4 and 1.88% for latex. Tire makers face both volume and price risk when weather-sensitive output weakens while large consuming markets continue drawing material. China was material at 617,000 tonnes of May consumption, although its imports fell 12.88% month on month. Watch ANRPC's next monthly revision to the 2026 forecasts and whether physical prices hold their May gains.

**Sources:** [ANRPC](https://www.anrpc.org/newsla/anrpc-releases-monthly-nr-statistical-report%2C-may-2026)

### Liberia bans exports of unprocessed natural rubber to force more domestic processing

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-26 | Government of Liberia*

President Joseph Boakai issued an executive order prohibiting exports of unprocessed natural rubber, including latex, coagulum, cup lump, tree lace and rubber scrap, for an indefinite period. Implementing regulations were due within 30 days. The policy moves value-chain intervention upstream: export access is conditioned on local processing rather than only farm production. The legal action is immediate, while the scale of new processing investment and farmer-market effects remain unproven. Liberia is an important producing market, so the order can redirect trade flows, processor utilization and procurement risk for international buyers. Watch the implementing regulations, enforcement date, licensed processing capacity and any farmer-price or export-volume response.

**Sources:** [Liberia Ministry of Information](https://www.micat.gov.lr/office/news/newsroom/press-release/president-boakai-issues-executive-order-banning-export)

### Review maps controlled routes from natural rubber to functional liquid oligomers

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-22 | Polymer Bulletin review authors*

A new open-access review compared mechanical, thermal, oxidative and photochemical routes for converting high-molecular-weight natural rubber into liquid natural rubber and telechelic derivatives, generally below 50,000 g/mol. Controlled chain scission can create hydroxyl-, carbonyl- and other functional end groups for coatings, blends, fillers, adhesives, foams and processing aids. The review highlights greener oxidants and photocatalytic approaches but also documents reaction-control, color, toxicity and purification limitations. This broadens natural rubber from bulk elastomer feedstock toward functional bio-based intermediates, while making process selectivity and cleanup the industrialization bottlenecks.

**Sources:** [Polymer Bulletin (Springer Nature)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00289-026-06549-5)

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-24 | Linglong Tire*

Tyre News reported on June 24 that Linglong's Yunnan sustainable-natural-rubber project was moving into a stronger demonstration phase through an ecological collection station in Xishuangbanna. According to the company claims carried by the report, the programme supports about 2,000 farmers and 10,000 hectares and combines smart weighing, data collection, farmer payments, wastewater treatment and forest-to-factory traceability. The model integrates physical collection, smallholder interaction, environmental controls and digital chain-of-custody at the first aggregation point rather than adding traceability only at the factory gate. Natural-rubber due diligence is difficult because supply is fragmented across smallholders. A functioning collection-station model could improve sourcing evidence for tire customers and regulators, but coverage figures, certification progress and outcomes remain company-origin claims. Watch FSC certification evidence, auditable lot-level data and measured farmer, biodiversity or wastewater outcomes.

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--marketing">Marketing &amp; Distribution</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-25 | TraXtion*

TraXtion launched TireSpec, a hardware-free website widget that uses computer vision and optical character recognition to read a smartphone photo of a tire sidewall, identify specifications and return replacement recommendations with inventory and pricing. The tire itself becomes the search input, removing vehicle, VIN and tire-size form fields while keeping the shopper inside the dealer or repairer's website. The workflow connects recognition directly to distributor inventory and quote generation. This is a concrete AI-enabled sales model for reducing friction between tire identification and a bookable replacement transaction. Accuracy and conversion improvement remain company claims without published field metrics. Watch named retailer deployments, quote-to-appointment conversion data and the two patent-pending applications cited by the company.

### China's tire volume rises through May while export value and May shipments weaken

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-06-23 | China tire industry; National Bureau of Statistics and Customs data*

CRIA's compilation of official statistics reported January-May rubber-tire casing output of 501.71 million units, up 1.9%. Tire exports reached 4.03 million tonnes, up 3.5%, but export value fell 2.4% to RMB67.408 billion; May export weight and value fell 4.9% and 10.4% year on year. Volume growth is not translating into value growth, and the May reversal suggests weaker shipment momentum or mix/price pressure. New pneumatic-tire exports were 3.87 million tonnes, up 3.3%, with value down 2.5%. For manufacturers and distributors, the divergence between tonnage and value is a stronger commercial signal than output alone and points to margin and market-mix pressure. Watch June customs volume, value and unit counts to see whether the May decline persists.

## R&D & Product Development

### Chinese and Thai researchers map natural rubber's built-in reinforcement system

*Published 2026-07-01 | CATAS Rubber Research Institute; Mahidol University*

**What changed:** Researchers proposed a multiscale framework explaining how the less-than-five-percent non-rubber fraction in natural rubber, including proteins, phospholipids, lipids, carbohydrates, and metals, can influence structure and performance from molecular interactions through colloidal organization to bulk behavior.

**Intelligence signal:** Natural rubber research is shifting from treating non-rubber constituents as impurities to engineering them as an intrinsic reinforcement system.

**Why it matters:** A better mechanistic model could improve control of thermal and oxidative stability, viscoelasticity, vulcanization kinetics, filler dispersion, and mechanical performance in tire compounds.

**Sources:** [CATAS Rubber Research Institute; Industrial Crops and Products](https://www.catasrri.cn/contents/3629/218952.html) | [second source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.indcrop.2026.123342)

## Materials & Supply Chain

### India recommends five-year duties on a tire-antioxidant feedstock from China

*Published 2026-06-29 | India Directorate General of Trade Remedies; Chinese 6PPD exporters*

**What changed:** India's trade-remedy authority issued a final affirmative recommendation covering imports of PX-13, a 6PPD-type tire antioxidant, from China, the European Union, South Korea, and Thailand. Proposed Chinese rates range from USD 511 to USD 930 per tonne, with named Sennics entities at the lower rate.

**Intelligence signal:** Trade remedies for tire chemicals are becoming producer-specific, increasing the value of localized supply and traceable exporter economics.

**Why it matters:** If implemented, the measures would change landed costs for a critical antidegradant and could reshape sourcing, pricing, and local-production decisions across the Indian tire supply chain.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association; India DGTR](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2071850906442371074) | [second source](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-06/FF%20NCV%20ENGLISH%20SIGNED.pdf)

## Manufacturing

### Guizhou Tyre moves the third stage of its OTR project into trial production

*Published 2026-07-03 | Guizhou Tyre*

**What changed:** Guizhou Tyre said the third-stage supporting environmental facilities for its 380,000-unit annual all-steel OTR radial tire intelligent-manufacturing project were completed on July 2, with trial production and testing scheduled from July 4 to October 4.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese OTR expansions are advancing through staged environmental acceptance and trial runs rather than a single plant-completion milestone.

**Why it matters:** The step brings another Chinese high-spec OTR program closer to usable capacity and creates a concrete commissioning window for equipment, process, and emissions performance.

**Sources:** [Guizhou Tyre](https://www.gztyre.com/news/ztnews-detail-10138.htm)

## Marketing & Distribution

### Triangle takes a five-pattern EV-tire range to Brazil's local channel

*Published 2026-07-01 | Triangle Tyre*

**What changed:** Triangle presented five EV-oriented passenger-tire patterns, TH801, TM802, TS602, TS603, and TV711, at two Brazilian industry events and said the range was localized for Latin American roads and use preferences.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire makers are using region-specific EV lineups to build distributor pull in Latin America rather than exporting a single global assortment.

**Why it matters:** Brazil is a strategic channel test for whether Chinese brands can combine EV positioning with local fit, dealer education, and replacement-market coverage.

**Sources:** [Triangle Tyre](https://www.triangle.com.cn/cn/index/pageview/catid/4/id/98.html)

## Sales & Customer Services

### Overseas factories and EV OE accounts emerge as Chinese tire makers' profit levers

*Published 2026-06-29 | Chinese listed tire manufacturers*

**What changed:** An industry-association analysis of 16 listed or quoted Chinese tire makers found nine overseas production bases and described overseas plants as disproportionately important to profit. It also documented a broader push toward higher-value original-equipment and new-energy-vehicle accounts.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire globalization is moving from export volume toward overseas manufacturing economics and higher-end OE mix.

**Why it matters:** The analysis suggests that location, tariff exposure, utilization, and vehicle-platform access are becoming as important as headline domestic capacity in explaining earnings quality.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2071469710357139457)

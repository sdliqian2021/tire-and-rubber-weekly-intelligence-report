## R&D & Product Development

### Pirelli agrees to acquire 24.99% of RIDEsense to combine physical and virtual tire sensors

*Published 2026-07-02 | Pirelli / RIDEsense*

**What changed:** Pirelli agreed to license RIDEsense's virtual-sensor technology and acquire a 24.99% equity stake, with an option to increase its holding to 100%. The partners plan to integrate RIDEsense algorithms with Pirelli Cyber Tyre's embedded physical sensors.

**Intelligence signal:** The smart-tire stack is expanding from tire-mounted hardware into hybrid physical-and-virtual sensing, using production-vehicle ECU data and physics-based algorithms to strengthen aquaplaning detection and add tire and vehicle diagnostics.

**Why it matters:** The deal gives Pirelli a faster route from a university spinout into ADAS and autonomous-driving functions, showing how tire makers are using licensing plus minority investment to capture software capability around the tire.

**Sources:** [Pirelli Newsroom](https://press.pirelli.com/pirelli-acquires-a-2499-stake-in-ridesense-a-start-up-spun-out-from-the-university-of-naples-federico-ii-and-the-megaride-group)

### South Korea funds a five-year push to raise recycled-carbon-black content in new tires

*Published 2026-07-02 | South Korea Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment; Korea Environmental Industry & Technology Institute*

**What changed:** South Korea's climate ministry and KEITI launched a 2026-2030 circular-use R&D program that allocates KRW 48 billion specifically to waste-tire technology. The work covers pretreatment before pyrolysis, higher-quality recovered carbon black, commercialization, and technologies intended to let new tires use more than 15% recycled carbon black.

**Intelligence signal:** Public circular-economy funding is moving beyond tire-derived fuel toward tire-to-material pathways, with an explicit recovered-carbon-black loading target tied to commercial tire production.

**Why it matters:** The program targets the quality barrier that the ministry says currently limits recycled material to roughly 5% in new tires and prepares the domestic value chain for future EU ecodesign requirements.

**Sources:** [South Korea Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment](https://me.go.kr/eng/web/board/read.do?boardId=1875600&boardMasterId=522&menuId=461)

### Sumitomo Rubber packages acquired AI into manufacturing and failure-prediction tools

*Published 2026-07-02 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries; Viaduct*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber said it would demonstrate two Viaduct-based AI solutions: one integrating development, production, sales, and quality data for cross-functional equipment root-cause analysis, and another learning from specifications, sensor, repair, and warranty data to identify failure patterns and estimate future risk.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-company data platforms are moving from isolated factory dashboards toward cross-lifecycle diagnostic models that connect manufacturing, product use, quality, and warranty signals.

**Why it matters:** If deployed successfully, the tools could shorten troubleshooting and support predictive maintenance and quality decisions, while creating a commercialization path for technology acquired with Viaduct in 2025.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/newsrelease/2026/sri/2026_069.html)

### Dunlop's ICE Pro deliberately trades broad balance for stronger ice performance

*Published 2026-07-01 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber announced an August 2026 launch for the 99-size ICE Pro winter range. It attributes the design to a new water-absorbing compound, low-temperature additive, moisture-retaining polymer, tread, and profile, and reports 25% better ice braking and 9% better ice cornering than Winter Maxx 03.

**Intelligence signal:** Product architecture is becoming more explicit about sacrificing some balanced performance to maximize a priority use case, here sustained adhesion on ice.

**Why it matters:** The launch tests whether customers will value specialized winter safety performance enough to accept the stated trade-off, while showing how compound aging is being addressed alongside new-tire grip.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/newsrelease/2026/sri/2026_066.html)

### Continental launches a mixed-use all-terrain tire with reinforced construction

*Published 2026-07-03 | Continental*

**What changed:** Continental introduced the CrossContact A/T² for SUVs, four-wheel-drive vehicles, EVs, and pickups. The tire combines increased tread depth and void ratio, reinforced sidewalls and off-shoulders, rigid blocks, self-cleaning elements, and a dedicated 4x4 compound; the initial range covers nine sizes from 15 to 17 inches.

**Intelligence signal:** All-terrain development is converging on one casing and compound package intended to balance paved-road predictability with traction and damage resistance off road.

**Why it matters:** The design illustrates how manufacturers are extending mixed-use products to EV and light-truck applications while managing classic trade-offs among grip, durability, noise, and handling.

**Sources:** [Continental](https://www.continental.com/en/press/press-releases/20260703-crosscontact-at2)


## Materials & Supply Chain

### Australia's mining-tire rules leave almost all OTR casings outside recycling loops

*Published 2026-07-01 | Australian Tyre Recyclers Association (ATRA)*

**What changed:** ATRA published a commissioned review of waste-mining-tire regulations and licensing in Western Australia, New South Wales and Queensland. It says current settings still permit disposal pathways that leave only about 2% of off-the-road mining tires recovered, despite installed domestic recycling capacity.

**Intelligence signal:** End-of-life OTR tires are emerging as a high-quality circular feedstock, but regulation and disposal economics—not processing technology alone—are determining whether their rubber and steel return to tire and materials supply chains.

**Why it matters:** Mine tires can weigh roughly 3-7 tonnes, so reforming on-site burial and recovery rules could unlock concentrated natural-rubber and steel feedstock for higher-value recycling, including tire-to-tire uses.

**Sources:** [Australian Tyre Recyclers Association](https://atra.asn.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ATRA-mining-tyre-legislation-report-FINAL1-copy.pdf) | [second source](https://atra.asn.au/news)

### Michelin completes Tex-Tech acquisition to deepen coated-textile and polymer-composite reach

*Published 2026-07-01 | Michelin; Tex-Tech Industries*

**What changed:** Michelin said it completed the acquisition of Tex-Tech after receiving required approvals. Tex-Tech designs and manufactures specialized textiles and coated fabrics, employs about 300 people, operates five U.S. plants and one U.K. plant, and reported 2025 revenue of USD 128 million.

**Intelligence signal:** Large tire groups are using adjacent polymer-composite acquisitions to diversify materials capabilities and end markets beyond tires.

**Why it matters:** Tex-Tech adds manufacturing sites and coated-fabric know-how to Michelin's Polymer Composite Solutions business and marks its third PCS acquisition in a year after Cooley and Flexitallic.

**Sources:** [Michelin](https://agngnconpm.cloudimg.io/v7/https%3A//dgaddcosprod.blob.core.windows.net/cxf-corporate/attachments/v6ufkcwg5i499yrjlaidkoos-michelin-completes-the-acquisition-of-tex-tech-industries-and-reinforces-its-pcs%C3%A2-business%C3%A2.pdf) | [second source](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/regulated-information?year=2026)

### Sumitomo Rubber moves to absorb its logistics subsidiary

*Published 2026-07-01 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries; SRI Logistics*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber's board resolved to absorb wholly owned freight-forwarder SRI Logistics, subject to a scheduled July 2 agreement and January 1, 2027 effective date. The company says the move will consolidate group logistics, improve operating efficiency, speed decisions, and create an optimized framework.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire manufacturers are bringing logistics governance closer to the operating company as volatility raises the value of faster network decisions and unified data.

**Why it matters:** The merger would internalize a subsidiary with JPY 3.98 billion of 2025 sales, but its real distribution benefit will depend on execution rather than the legal simplification alone.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries stock-exchange filing](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/english/newsrelease/2026/sri/dvql4p000011pxoy-att/2026_disclosure0701.pdf)

### Tolins subsidiary agrees to buy reclaimed-rubber machinery for backward integration

*Published 2026-07-01 | Tolins Tyres; Terra Rubber*

**What changed:** Tolins Tyres disclosed that its wholly owned Terra Rubber subsidiary signed a July 1 agreement to acquire identified plant and machinery from Cochin Reclaim and Rubbers for capacity expansion and backward integration. Equipment is to be removed within 60 days, with title passing after payment and removal.

**Intelligence signal:** Smaller tire and tread-rubber producers are using second-hand or transferred assets to add upstream reclaimed-rubber capability without buying an entire company.

**Why it matters:** The deal could increase Terra Rubber's supply control and recycling capacity, although its economic significance cannot be assessed without purchase value, equipment list, or incremental output.

**Sources:** [Tolins Tyres BSE filing; ScanX](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/0dcc6134-6015-44c6-b6ba-106fc6991a77.pdf) | [second source](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/tolins-tyres-subsidiary-terra-rubber-buys-plant-for-capacity-expansion/44443491)


## Manufacturing

### Continental signs ContiTech sale and sets a path to become a pure-play tire maker

*Published 2026-07-04 | Continental; Lone Star Funds; ContiTech*

**What changed:** Continental announced a signed agreement to sell ContiTech to a Lone Star affiliate for EUR 4.0 billion plus up to EUR 250 million in performance-based components. It expects about EUR 3.1 billion of cash after transferred liabilities; all ContiTech operations worldwide would transfer once approvals are obtained.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-sector conglomerates are separating industrial rubber and thermoplastic operations from tire manufacturing to sharpen capital allocation and strategic focus.

**Why it matters:** The transaction would create an independent 22,000-employee materials company with about EUR 4.4 billion in 2025 sales and turn Continental into a pure-play tire manufacturer, changing ownership across a major industrial-rubber supply network.

**Sources:** [Continental](https://cdn.continental.com/en/press/press-releases/continental-ct-2026)

### Continental plans its first owned wind farm for direct tire-plant power

*Published 2026-07-02 | Continental*

**What changed:** Continental approved three wind turbines about eight kilometers from its Korbach tire plant. With existing solar, the project is intended to cover around two-thirds of plant electricity demand and generate about 55 GWh annually; permits are obtained, construction is scheduled in 2026, and commissioning is planned roughly 18 months later.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire plants are moving from renewable certificates and purchased electricity toward owned, site-linked generation as both a decarbonization and energy-cost hedge.

**Why it matters:** The mid-double-digit-million-euro project offers a measurable test of whether direct generation can lower exposure to volatile power markets and serve as a repeatable model for energy-intensive mixing and component production.

**Sources:** [Continental](https://www.continental.com/en/press/press-releases/wind-farm-korbach)

### Guizhou Tyre moves the third stage of its OTR project into trial production

*Published 2026-07-03 | Guizhou Tyre*

**What changed:** Guizhou Tyre said the third-stage supporting environmental facilities for its 380,000-unit annual all-steel OTR radial tire intelligent-manufacturing project were completed on July 2, with trial production and testing scheduled from July 4 to October 4.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese OTR expansions are advancing through staged environmental acceptance and trial runs rather than a single plant-completion milestone.

**Why it matters:** The step brings another Chinese high-spec OTR program closer to usable capacity and creates a concrete commissioning window for equipment, process, and emissions performance.

**Sources:** [Guizhou Tyre](https://www.gztyre.com/news/ztnews-detail-10138.htm)

### Apollo Tyres closes tire production at its Enschede plant

*Published 2026-06-30 | Apollo Tyres*

**What changed:** Apollo Tyres filed that its Netherlands subsidiary discontinued tire production and production-related operations at Enschede at the close of business on June 30 after obtaining works-council and supervisory-board approvals.

**Intelligence signal:** European tire manufacturing footprints continue to be consolidated as producers shift output away from higher-cost or less-specialized sites.

**Why it matters:** The filing converts a previously proposed closure into a completed manufacturing event and removes Enschede production from Apollo's European network, with implications for sourcing, labor, and capacity allocation.

**Sources:** [Apollo Tyres stock-exchange filing](https://corporate.apollotyres.com/content/dam/orbit/apollo-corporate/investors/announcements/2026-27/Disclsoure%20as%20per%20Reg%2030%20of%20SEBI%20LODR%20Enschede%20Update%20-%20June%2030%2C%202026.pdf)


## Marketing & Distribution

### Kumho turns four light-truck products into a terrain-based buying ladder

*Published 2026-06-29 | Kumho Tire U.S.A.*

**What changed:** Kumho organized its U.S. Road Venture light-truck portfolio into four differentiated steps: HT for highway, AT52 for all-terrain, RT for rugged terrain, and MT71 for mud terrain. The release pairs each use case with warranties and performance cues; the HT carries 3PMSF and warranties of 75,000 miles for P-metric and 50,000 miles for LT-metric sizes.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire makers are simplifying complex SUV and pickup portfolios into use-case ladders that make trade-offs more legible at the dealer and consumer decision point.

**Why it matters:** Clear portfolio architecture can reduce selection friction in the large U.S. light-truck market while giving dealers a coherent path from highway to severe off-road use.

**Sources:** [Kumho Tire U.S.A.; PR Newswire](https://kumhotireusa.com/press-room/3247) | [second source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kumho-tire-simplifies-light-truck-tire-selection-with-road-venture-lineup-302812879.html)

### Dunlop makes a heritage mini-bike tire exclusive to its official online store

*Published 2026-06-29 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber said sales of a TT100 mini-bike tire would begin July 1 exclusively through Dunlop's official Japanese online tire store. The 3.50-8 46J front/rear product uses the historic T100 tread pattern and targets wheel-customization and tubeless-conversion users.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire makers are using direct e-commerce exclusives to serve small enthusiast niches that may not justify broad dealer inventory.

**Why it matters:** The launch provides a low-volume test of direct assortment expansion, customer discovery, and heritage-brand monetization outside conventional replacement distribution.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/newsrelease/2026/sri/2026_064.html)


## Sales & Customer Services

### Michelin adds conversational AI to fleet operations and tire-maintenance queries

*Published 2026-06-29 | Michelin Connected Fleet*

**What changed:** Michelin integrated an AI assistant into MyConnectedFleet so managers can query live fleet data and generate operational answers, including which trucks require tire maintenance, vehicle downtime, fuel use, and driver-efficiency reports. Michelin says the tool operates in a closed environment and is available in ten European and North American countries.

**Intelligence signal:** Fleet service is moving from dashboards and manual reports toward natural-language decision support tied to maintenance and operating data.

**Why it matters:** The assistant can make tire needs more visible within broader fleet workflows, potentially accelerating service decisions across a platform Michelin says covers 660,000 vehicles and 70,000 customers.

**Sources:** [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/michelin-connected-fleet-ai-assistant-integrated) | [second source](https://connectedfleet.michelin.com/solution/ai-assistant)

### Overseas factories and EV OE accounts emerge as Chinese tire makers' profit levers

*Published 2026-06-29 | Chinese listed tire manufacturers*

**What changed:** An industry-association analysis of 16 listed or quoted Chinese tire makers found nine overseas production bases and described overseas plants as disproportionately important to profit. It also documented a broader push toward higher-value original-equipment and new-energy-vehicle accounts.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire globalization is moving from export volume toward overseas manufacturing economics and higher-end OE mix.

**Why it matters:** The analysis suggests that location, tariff exposure, utilization, and vehicle-platform access are becoming as important as headline domestic capacity in explaining earnings quality.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2071469710357139457)

### Goodyear locks in ELMS supply through 2029 as racing tires carry up to 66% sustainable materials

*Published 2026-07-02 | Goodyear; Automobile Club de l'Ouest; European Le Mans Series*

**What changed:** Goodyear and ACO extended Goodyear's exclusive tire-supply role for the ELMS LMP2 and LMGT3 categories through 2029. Goodyear said its current LMGT3 specification contains up to 66% sustainable materials and that its latest LMP2 slick completed five consecutive Le Mans stints, close to 700 km, while maintaining competitive pace and degradation control.

**Intelligence signal:** Long-term control-tire contracts are combining customer lock-in, cost control, and a track-development pathway for higher renewable and recycled content.

**Why it matters:** The deal gives Goodyear a multi-year customer and test platform across two strategically important endurance categories while linking sustainable-material claims to demanding use conditions.

**Sources:** [Goodyear EMEA Newsroom; European Le Mans Series](https://news.goodyear.eu/goodyear-and-aco-extend-european-le-mans-series-partnership-until-2029) | [second source](https://www.europeanlemansseries.com/en/news/goodyear-and-aco-extend-european-le-mans-series-partnership-until-2029/13655)

### Michelin flags a split tire market as premium replacement demand offsets OE weakness

*Published 2026-07-02 | Michelin*

**What changed:** In its H1 pre-close script, Michelin described global passenger and light-truck OE demand as down about 3% through May while replacement was roughly flat, with China replacement strong and U.S. demand declining. It said its own second-quarter tire volumes were broadly stable and premium Michelin replacement sales offset pressure on lower tiers from cheap imports.

**Intelligence signal:** Global tire demand is fragmenting by region, channel, and price tier rather than moving in one cycle, increasing the value of premium mix and local inventory visibility.

**Why it matters:** The management signal helps distributors and suppliers distinguish weak OE and North American conditions from stronger Chinese replacement and mining demand, while highlighting ongoing imported-tier pressure in Europe and the United States.

**Sources:** [Michelin pre-close call script](https://agngnconpm.cloudimg.io/v7/https%3A//dgaddcosprod.blob.core.windows.net/cxf-corporate/attachments/fzlsr7bo0gy0tt70ei2ciwf5-michelin-pre-close-call-h1-2026-for-sell-side-analysts.pdf) | [second source](https://www.michelin.com/en/investors/regulatory-information)

### Sumitomo Rubber sets a 6% average increase for Japan replacement tires

*Published 2026-06-29 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber announced an average 6% increase in distributor shipment prices from September 1 for domestic replacement-market Dunlop and Falken tires, tubes, and flaps. It cited continuing increases in raw materials, labor, logistics, and energy costs.

**Intelligence signal:** Replacement-tire pricing is broadening from raw-material pass-through to recovery of labor, freight, and energy inflation.

**Why it matters:** The increase is a direct market signal for Japanese dealers and fleets and may test brand pricing power if consumer demand remains soft or competitors move differently.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/newsrelease/2026/sri/2026_070.html)

### Bridgestone wins Yamaha YZF-R7 original-equipment fitment for BATTLAX S23

*Published 2026-06-29 | Bridgestone; Yamaha Motor*

**What changed:** Bridgestone announced that Yamaha selected BATTLAX HYPERSPORT S23 tires for the new YZF-R7: 120/70ZR17 front and 180/55ZR17 rear. Bridgestone attributes the fitment to optimized shoulder-groove ratio and block rigidity plus its Pulse Groove water-drainage pattern.

**Intelligence signal:** Motorcycle OE programs are combining early vehicle-development collaboration with differentiated wet-drainage and handling technologies to secure premium platform access.

**Why it matters:** The win gives Bridgestone a visible validation point for BATTLAX technology and embeds the brand at new-vehicle sale, which can influence later replacement demand.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone](https://www.bridgestone.com/corporate/news/2026062901.html) | [second source](https://www.yamaha-motor.eu/gb/en/motorcycles/supersport/pdp/r7-2026)

### USTMA pairs tire-maintenance guidance with a new Full Circle public platform

*Published 2026-06-29 | U.S. Tire Manufacturers Association*

**What changed:** USTMA opened National Tire Safety Week with a monthly and pre-trip Pressure, Alignment, Rotation, and Tread checklist and launched Full Circle, a public storytelling platform organized around safety, sustainability, innovation, economic impact, and advocacy. Eleven U.S. tire-maker members support the campaign.

**Intelligence signal:** Industry aftercare communication is shifting from a one-week safety reminder toward an always-on platform linking maintenance with the tire lifecycle and industry value.

**Why it matters:** A shared message can reinforce inspection and maintenance behavior across brands and channels, though campaign reach and resulting consumer action will determine practical impact.

**Sources:** [U.S. Tire Manufacturers Association](https://www.ustires.org/newsroom/ustma-kicks-national-tire-safety-week-june-29-july-4) | [second source](https://www.ustires.org/fullcircle)

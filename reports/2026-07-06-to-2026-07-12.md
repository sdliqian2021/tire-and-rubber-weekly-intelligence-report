## R&D & Product Development

### Bridgestone moves AirFree from trials into a regular autonomous transit service

*Published 2026-07-07 | Bridgestone; Higashiomi City*

**What changed:** Bridgestone and Higashiomi City announced Japan's first practical deployment of the airless AirFree tire in the city's regularly operated low-speed autonomous mobility service, beginning July 8 after earlier limited municipal trials.

**Intelligence signal:** Airless tires are crossing from demonstrations into a constrained commercial-service environment where uptime and reduced pressure maintenance have clear operating value.

**Why it matters:** The deployment is small and low speed, but it creates real service data and a concrete adoption pathway for autonomous community transport before broader road applications.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone Japan; Higashiomi City](https://www.bridgestone.co.jp/corporate/news/2026070701.html) | [second source](https://www.city.higashiomi.shiga.jp/kurashi_tetsuzuki/koutsuu/1002061/index.html)

### EU safety rules make worn-tire performance a vehicle-market requirement

*Published 2026-07-08 | European Union vehicle and tire manufacturers*

**What changed:** The European Commission confirmed that the next phase of the General Safety Regulation took effect on July 7 for newly registered passenger cars and vans, including new tests for worn tires.

**Intelligence signal:** Regulation is shifting tire validation from new-tire performance toward whole-life wet-safety behavior, forcing tread, compound and test-development choices to account for progressive wear.

**Why it matters:** A lifecycle safety requirement changes the product-development gate for the EU market and raises the value of worn-state testing, durable wet grip and traceable compliance evidence.

**Sources:** [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-cars-safer-roads-new-rules-take-effect-2026-07-08_en) | [second source](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/07/eu-safety-rules-extend-to-worn-tyre-wet-grip)

### ZC Rubber and HIT win a state award for an integrated tire technology stack

*Published 2026-07-08 | ZC Rubber, Harbin Institute of Technology and partners*

**What changed:** The High-performance Tire Key Technology and Its Green Intelligent Manufacturing project received second prize in China's State Science and Technology Progress Awards.

**Intelligence signal:** HIT disclosed a multi-information-fusion tire design method, digital analysis and optimization software, SiO2/C and CNT/C micro-nano composites, green manufacturing processes and core equipment, including aviation-tire application.

**Why it matters:** This is a rare full-stack advance spanning materials, digital design, software, equipment and production rather than treating product and factory R&D separately.

**Sources:** [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://news.hit.edu.cn/2026/0708/c11508a242822/page.htm) | [second source](https://www.most.gov.cn/cxfw/kjjlcx/kjjl2025/202607/t20260708_196987.html)

### Toyo patent targets lower heat generation from reclaimed rubber without sacrificing fracture performance

*Published 2026-07-09 | Toyo Tire Corporation*

**What changed:** A Toyo Tire patent application published July 9 describes tire compounds containing 1-15 mass percent reclaimed rubber with controlled natural-rubber content, gel fraction and molecular-weight distribution. The application targets lower heat generation while limiting deterioration in fracture properties, particularly for heavy-duty base treads.

**Intelligence signal:** The formulation uses mechanically desulfurized truck-and-bus tread rubber and defines measurable polymer, tensile, elongation, ash and carbon-black ranges to manage the performance variability that constrains reclaimed-rubber use.

**Why it matters:** A workable balance between heat build-up and durability would make reclaimed rubber more credible in performance-sensitive tire components, but the publication establishes an intellectual-property claim rather than commercial readiness.

**Sources:** [USPTO patent publication](https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/applications/19436218) | [second source](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260193451)

### Triangle Tire publishes RFID-barcode binding and rejection patent application

*Published 2026-07-07 | Triangle Tire*

**What changed:** Patent application CN122347166A was published for automatically linking an RFID tag embedded in a green tire with its production barcode after curing.

**Intelligence signal:** Panoramic barcode scanning and RFID reading send both identities to MES; rule validation creates a one-to-one record and failed matches trigger automatic line rejection.

**Why it matters:** The method closes a traceability gap between the physical tire, embedded digital identity and manufacturing record, supporting automated quality control and future product passports.

**Sources:** [Spanish Patent and Trademark Office technology alert](https://www.oepm.es/en/informacion-tecnologica/vigilancia-tecnologica/alertas-tecnologicas/alerta?id=851&page=31) | [second source](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN122347166A/zh)

### China tire forum points to bio-materials, electric curing, and AI-assisted R&D

*Published 2026-07-06 | CRIA forum participants including Linglong, Aeolus, Double Coin, and suppliers*

**What changed:** CRIA published technical disclosures from its Global Tire Technology Forum covering epoxidized natural rubber, itaconic-acid bio-rubber, renewable fillers, low-hysteresis carbon black, AI material simulation, laser wear scanning, and electric curing.

**Intelligence signal:** Sustainability work is moving from isolated substitutions toward coordinated changes in compounds, curing, testing, and digital formulation.

**Why it matters:** A portfolio view shows which Chinese technologies may combine into lower-carbon tires, while individual performance claims still require independent validation.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2074024351431233538)

## Materials & Supply Chain

### Michelin and Axens prepare global licensing of bio-based 5-HMF chemistry

*Published 2026-07-08 | Michelin ResiCare, Axens and IFPEN*

**What changed:** Michelin ResiCare and Axens entered exclusive discussions to industrialize and commercialize Michelin-IFPEN technology for producing bio-based 5-HMF. Michelin's first French unit is designed for approximately 3,000 tonnes per year and is scheduled to start in early 2027.

**Intelligence signal:** A tire maker is moving a renewable platform molecule from joint R&D into a licensable industrial process that can replace some fossil-derived inputs, including formaldehyde, in resins and polymers.

**Why it matters:** The partnership connects Michelin's materials research with Axens' engineering and licensing network, potentially extending tire-sector bio-material innovation into a broader global chemicals supply chain.

**Sources:** [MICHELIN ResiCare](https://resicare.michelin.com/news/michelin-resicare-axens-bio-based-5-hmf)

### Bekaert and CITIC Special Steel launch closed-loop tire-steel project

*Published 2026-07-08 | Bekaert and CITIC Pacific Special Steel*

**What changed:** The companies signed a technical-cooperation agreement to test whether steel recovered from end-of-life tires can be remade into wire rod and then new tire-reinforcement products.

**Intelligence signal:** The early proof of concept combines joint studies, industrial trials and material evaluation for high shares of closed-loop steel without sacrificing required quality or performance.

**Why it matters:** Successful tire-to-tire steel recovery would move recycling beyond downcycling, reduce virgin-steel demand and give tire makers a traceable circular reinforcement input.

**Sources:** [Bekaert](https://www.bekaert.com/en/about-us/news-room/news/2026/bekaert-and-citic-special-steel-sign-strategic-cooperation-agree) | [second source](https://www.european-rubber-journal.com/article/2099421/chinese-steel-group-bekaert-launch-close-loop-recycled-tire-steel-project)

### Chinese PA66 reinforcement reaches industrial-scale aviation-tire application

*Published 2026-07-10 | Shuguang Rubber Institute, Shenma Industrial, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University and partners*

**What changed:** A project on continuous-polymerization melt direct-spinning of high-performance PA66 industrial filament and its use in premium tires received a national science and technology progress second prize.

**Intelligence signal:** The team linked industrial-scale high-viscosity PA66 direct spinning to cord fabric adapted for aircraft tires, reporting higher load and impact performance with lower tire weight.

**Why it matters:** Localized, safety-critical aviation-tire reinforcement strengthens China's materials supply chain and demonstrates transfer from polymer process engineering into a validated tire application.

**Sources:** [Sinochem Haohua / Shuguang Rubber Institute](https://haohua.sinochem.com/haohua/xwzx/gsxw/2026/7/I1525064924701655040.html) | [second source](https://www.most.gov.cn/cxfw/kjjlcx/kjjl2025/202607/t20260708_196987.html)

### Chinese tire export orders weaken under trade and freight pressure

*Published 2026-07-07 | Chinese PCR and TBR producers*

**What changed:** Longzhong's surveyed producers reported weaker export-order direction for both semi-steel and all-steel tires amid trade barriers, freight costs, and soft domestic demand.

**Intelligence signal:** Export friction is translating into production restraint and attempts to redirect Chinese tire sales toward alternative markets.

**Why it matters:** The direction affects utilization and near-term demand for rubber, carbon black, chemicals, reinforcement, and logistics, even though the visible survey lacks a complete sampling method.

**Sources:** [Longzhong Information](https://www.oilchem.net/26-0707-11-afbbd8def131d482.html)

## Marketing & Distribution

### EU duties reset the economics of Chinese PCR and light-truck tire exports

*Published 2026-07-09 | Chinese tire exporters and the European Commission*

**What changed:** The EU imposed definitive anti-dumping duties of 4.3% to 45.3% on China-origin passenger-car and light-lorry tires; the measures took effect on July 8 and a parallel anti-subsidy case remains open.

**Intelligence signal:** Trade barriers are accelerating localized production, market diversification, higher-value products and lower dependence on price-led direct exports from China.

**Why it matters:** China supplied almost 93 million covered tires worth more than EUR 2.5 billion in 2024, so the duties have immediate consequences for pricing, margins, channels and volume.

**Sources:** [European Commission](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-imposes-anti-dumping-duties-passenger-car-and-light-lorry-tyres-china-2026-07-09_en) | [second source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT?uri=CELEX%3A32026R1540)

**Why it matters:** Bringing products, digital services and technical advice directly to fleets, dealers and logistics companies is a route-to-market model built around total cost of ownership and ongoing service rather than product display alone.

### Bartrums puts Michelin's four-life tire model into a 450-asset fleet contract

*Published 2026-07-06 | Michelin, Bartrums and Tructyre*

**What changed:** Bartrums adopted a full-service contract with Michelin Connected Solutions across more than 450 trucks and trailers, using daily tire management, regrooving and Remix retreads; the operator reports an estimated 55.7 tonnes of CO2 savings in the first 11 months.

**Intelligence signal:** The offer combines tire-as-a-service with casing management: a new tire is regrooved, retreaded at Michelin's Stoke Remix plant and potentially regrooved again, linking fleet service, circular material use and domestic retread manufacturing.

**Why it matters:** The case shows how tire makers can create recurring service value beyond the initial sale while keeping casings in use longer; Michelin says each retread requires about 50 kg less raw material than a new truck tire.

**Sources:** [Michelin / Garnett Keeler PR](https://gk.news/michelin-truck-bus/press-release/michelins-sustainable-approach-proves-perfect-fit-for-bartrums-ambitions) | [second source](https://www.tyrenews.co.uk/news/michelin-tyre-contract-helps-bartrums-cut-fleet-co2)

### Japan's June replacement-tire rebound outpaces its OE recovery

*Published 2026-07-09 | Japan Automobile Tyre Manufacturers Association*

**What changed:** JATMA's June data show total replacement-tire sales of 4.949 million units, up 21.6% year over year, including a 23.8% increase in passenger-car tires. OE sales totaled 3.676 million units, up 10.2%, while first-half totals were only 2.5% higher for replacement tires and 2.4% higher for OE tires.

**Intelligence signal:** The simultaneous monthly rebound across passenger, light-truck and truck-and-bus categories contrasts with much slower first-half growth, indicating a sharp June acceleration rather than uniformly strong demand throughout 2026.

**Why it matters:** The split between a 21.6% replacement increase and 10.2% OE increase provides a direct demand signal for manufacturers, distributors and service networks operating in Japan.

**Sources:** [Japan Automobile Tyre Manufacturers Association](https://www.jatma.or.jp/toukei/download.php?id=MS8xXzIwMjZfMDYucGRm) | [second source](https://www.jatma.or.jp/toukei/download.php?id=Mi8yXzIwMjZfMDYucGRm)

## R&D & Product Development

### Bridgestone's W909 combines a lower-heat casing with two-retread winter service

*Published 2026-07-13 | Bridgestone*

**What changed:** Bridgestone announced the W909 truck-and-bus studless winter tire for a September 1 launch in 12 sizes. It is the company's first truck-and-bus winter tire using its Low Thermal Case and the first in that range designed to support two retreads across a broad customer base.

**Intelligence signal:** Commercial winter-tire design is linking casing heat control, new foamed rubber, wear-resistant patterning, and multiple retread lives rather than optimizing ice performance alone.

**Why it matters:** Longer casing life and less irregular wear could reduce fleet downtime, replacement cost, and material demand if the published test gains carry into varied customer operations.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone Japan](https://www.bridgestone.co.jp/corporate/news/2026071301.html)

### Continental adds a long-haul drive retread built around fuel and wear efficiency

*Published 2026-07-13 | Continental Tires*

**What changed:** Continental introduced the ContiTread HDL 5 EP long-haul drive-axle retread with a five-rib tread, an optimized footprint and V-shaped grooves, a new compound, and three-dimensional sipes. The company lists three tread widths and a 21/32-inch tread depth for the North American market.

**Intelligence signal:** Retread product development is increasingly combining casing reuse with application-specific tread geometry and compound tuning aimed at fleet fuel and wear economics.

**Why it matters:** A stronger long-haul retread offer can extend casing value and lower fleet tire cost, although its claimed efficiency and durability advantages still need independent field data.

**Sources:** [Continental Tires](https://www.continental-tires.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/truck-and-bus/contitread-hdl-5-ep) | [second source](https://www.continental-tires.com/us/en/products/truck/retreads/contitread-hdl-5-ep)

### Ikon schedules its first in-house UHP platform for a 2027 launch

*Published 2026-07-15 | Ikon Tyres*

**What changed:** Ikon Tyres presented Autograph Ultra 5, its first premium ultra-high-performance summer tire developed in-house, with consumer sales planned for 2027. The company describes a 54-size platform using a revised textile-cord layout, a low-permeability inner liner, and modified rubber compounds and additives.

**Intelligence signal:** A regional manufacturer is moving from inherited product assets toward a proprietary, export-oriented UHP platform with its own architecture and compounding choices.

**Why it matters:** The program could increase competition in performance tires across Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, and later export markets, but certification and independent testing remain gating steps.

**Sources:** [Ikon Tyres; Tyrepress](https://www.ikontyres.ru/o-kompanii/publikatsii/press-relizy/2026/productRelease/ikon-tyres-predstavila-pervuyu-premialnuyu-uhp-shinu-sobstvennoy-razrabotki) | [second source](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/07/ikon-enters-uhp-segment)

### Michelin patent pairs a highly saturated diene elastomer with azosilane-coupled silica

*Published 2026-07-16 | Michelin*

**What changed:** The USPTO published a Michelin patent application for a rubber composition combining a highly saturated ethylene and 1,3-diene copolymer, a silica reinforcing filler, an azosilane coupling agent, and a vulcanization system for tire applications. Michelin's examples report higher stiffness together with reduced hysteresis for the tested formulations.

**Intelligence signal:** Elastomer architecture and the filler-coupling package are being co-designed to pursue stiffness and energy-loss improvements rather than optimizing either component alone.

**Why it matters:** If the laboratory balance survives processing, durability, and scale-up, it could support lower-loss tread compounds without surrendering stiffness, but a patent publication does not establish product adoption.

**Sources:** [United States Patent and Trademark Office; Justia Patents](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/20260200268) | [second source](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260200268)

### Thailand's tire association joins TIP's global research network

*Published 2026-07-14 | Tire Industry Project; Thailand Automotive Tyre Manufacturers Association*

**What changed:** The Tire Industry Project admitted the Thailand Automotive Tyre Manufacturers Association as its ninth affiliate member, extending the research and sustainability platform into a major Southeast Asian tire-producing market.

**Intelligence signal:** Industry research on end-of-life tires, circularity, raw materials, and environmental performance is being federated through national associations as well as individual manufacturers.

**Why it matters:** Thailand's participation can improve the geographic reach and local relevance of joint research, although membership alone does not guarantee new standards or implementation.

**Sources:** [Tire Industry Project](https://tireindustryproject.org/news/tire-industry-project-welcomes-thailand-automotive-tyre-manufacturers-association-as-ninth-affiliate-member)


## Materials & Supply Chain

### Genan agrees to sell Houston as Liberty consolidates its Gulf Coast position

*Published 2026-07-16 | Liberty Tire Recycling; Genan*

**What changed:** Liberty Tire Recycling announced three Gulf Coast acquisitions, including Genan's U.S. subsidiary and Houston tire-recycling plant; Genan described its transaction as an agreement to divest the facility and refocus on Europe. If completed, the transactions would bring regional collection and recycled-rubber processing assets under one U.S. operator.

**Intelligence signal:** End-of-life tire collection and material processing are consolidating regionally as global recyclers sharpen their geographic portfolios.

**Why it matters:** Integrated collection and processing may improve feedstock control and market reach for recycled rubber, but ownership consolidation alone does not prove higher recovery volumes or better economics.

**Sources:** [Liberty Tire Recycling; Genan](https://libertytire.com/About/News/Liberty-Tire-Recycling-Strengthens-Gulf-Coast-Operations-and-Recycling-Efforts-with-Strategic-Acquisitions) | [second source](https://genan.com/knowledge/news/genan-divests-us-plant)

### Pirelli patent application details functionalized-lignin compounds for vehicle tires

*Published 2026-07-16 | Pirelli Tyre S.p.A.*

**What changed:** The USPTO published Pirelli application US20260201168A1 covering tires and vulcanizable compounds that use a diene-rubber/functionalized-lignin predispersion, reinforcing filler, and vulcanizing agent. The application also claims a mechanochemical route for functionalizing lignin.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-material R&D is adapting renewable lignin through chemical functionalization and controlled predispersion so it can participate more effectively in rubber compounds.

**Why it matters:** The application targets lower hysteresis and improved mechanical properties while substituting a renewable biopolymer for part of conventional reinforcing inputs, but these are patent-disclosure claims rather than evidence of commercial deployment.

**Sources:** [U.S. Patent and Trademark Office; Justia Patents](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/20260201168) | [second source](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260201168)

### U.S. Commerce advances a preliminary review of China PVLT tire duties

*Published 2026-07-17 | U.S. Department of Commerce*

**What changed:** Commerce published preliminary results of its 2024-2025 antidumping-duty administrative review for passenger-vehicle and light-truck tires from China. It preliminarily found reviewed producers or exporters sold below normal value and rescinded the review for 19 companies, while inviting comments before final results.

**Intelligence signal:** Trade-remedy exposure remains company-specific and administratively fluid, adding a regulatory cost layer to cross-border tire sourcing.

**Why it matters:** Importers and manufacturers need to model provisional company treatment and cash-deposit risk, but commercial effects cannot be treated as final until Commerce completes the review.

**Sources:** [U.S. Department of Commerce via GovInfo; Federal Register mirror](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-17/html/2026-14423.htm) | [second source](https://regulations.justia.com/regulations/fedreg/2026/07/17/2026-14423.html)

### Hankook turns spent curing bladders into a designed secondary-material stream

*Published 2026-07-14 | Hankook Tire*

**What changed:** Hankook said its Bladder Upcycling Series won Gold at the 2026 A' Design Award. The re:move project repurposes spent tire-curing bladders into furniture, bricks, and lifestyle goods rather than treating the factory consumables solely as waste.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-factory circularity is expanding beyond end-of-life tires toward higher-value uses for process consumables and manufacturing by-products.

**Why it matters:** The project broadens the recoverable material map inside tire plants, but its supply-chain relevance depends on repeatable processing, demand, economics, and disclosed scale.

**Sources:** [Hankook Tire](https://www.hankooktire.com/global/en/company/media-list/media-detail.633550.html?contentType=&tabCode=)

### Coats packages compact 115-volt balancing into a right-sized service-bay platform

**What changed:** Coats expanded its RC Series with RC 450 and RC 550 compact wheel balancers designed to pair with its tire changers and operate on 115-volt power. The two models provide different levels of dimensional data entry and are specified for common passenger and light-truck applications.

## Manufacturing

### China proposes tighter and broader air-emission controls for tire plants

*Published 2026-07-13 | China Ministry of Ecology and Environment*

**What changed:** China's environment ministry opened consultation on a draft national rubber-products air-pollutant emission standard. The proposal broadens process and pollutant coverage, explicitly encompasses tire manufacturing, tightens organized-emission limits, and adds fugitive-emission and monitoring requirements.

**Intelligence signal:** Environmental control is moving from narrow end-of-pipe limits toward plant-wide process, fugitive-emission, and monitoring obligations.

**Why it matters:** If adopted, the standard could require tire plants to reassess capture systems, treatment capacity, monitoring points, operating records, and capital plans.

**Sources:** [China Ministry of Ecology and Environment; China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk06/202607/t20260713_1161579.html) | [second source](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2076859217608806401)

### CEAT approves INR 12.05 billion for two-wheeler tire capacity expansion

*Published 2026-07-16 | CEAT*

**What changed:** CEAT's board approved an INR 12.05 billion investment to expand two-wheeler tire manufacturing capacity as the company reported first-quarter revenue growth alongside a sharp profit decline. The authorization signals continued capacity investment despite raw-material and foreign-exchange pressure on near-term earnings.

**Intelligence signal:** High utilization and demand expectations are sustaining capacity commitments even when input costs and currency movements compress margins.

**Why it matters:** The program is a material India two-wheeler capacity signal, but its competitive effect depends on project phasing, site execution, incremental output, and market absorption.

**Sources:** [CEAT BSE filing; ET Auto](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/c1481867-9e3c-4e9e-ba9f-93efc8a7637a.pdf) | [second source](https://auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/tyres/ceat-q1-net-profit-drops-96-to-4-crore/132443811)

### VMI patent couples tire-building drum and separator motion to improve splice consistency

*Published 2026-07-16 | VMI Holland B.V.*

**What changed:** USPTO application US20260200192A1 describes tire-building equipment that repositions the drum working surface to align the leading and trailing ends of an uncured tire component with the splicer track while allowing the separator body to follow that correction motion.

**Intelligence signal:** The design introduces coordinated motion compensation during green-tire building: drum position, the separator and optionally splicer-wheel speed respond to end-position deviation instead of treating the splice geometry as fixed.

**Why it matters:** More consistent butt splices could reduce a source of green-tire build variation and improve automated process control, although the filing provides no production-line validation, defect-rate reduction or cycle-time result.

**Sources:** [United States Patent and Trademark Office](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/20260200192) | [second source](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260200192)

### U.S. tire output rises into June while synthetic-rubber output stays flat

*Published 2026-07-17 | U.S. tire and synthetic-rubber manufacturers*

**What changed:** The Federal Reserve's July G.17 release put the seasonally adjusted U.S. tire production index at 80.7 in June, up from 80.0 in May, while the synthetic-rubber index remained at 79.8. The tire series has risen from 73.0 in January, although June is preliminary.

**Intelligence signal:** Downstream tire production improved while the upstream synthetic-rubber series did not move in the latest monthly reading.

**Why it matters:** The divergence is a useful utilization and procurement signal, but it cannot establish causation because synthetic rubber serves other markets and the indices measure output rather than physical tire or rubber volumes.

**Sources:** [Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System; FRED](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/20260717/table1b_sup.htm) | [second source](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/IPG32621N)


## Marketing & Distribution

### Big Brand's Belle Tire deal would create a 530-plus-site service network

*Published 2026-07-16 | Big Brand Tire & Service; Belle Tire*

**What changed:** Big Brand Tire & Service announced a definitive agreement to acquire Belle Tire, with closing expected in the third quarter subject to customary conditions. The companies say the combination would operate more than 530 locations and generate more than USD 1.5 billion in revenue.

**Intelligence signal:** Independent tire retail and automotive service platforms are consolidating across U.S. regions to gain purchasing scale, brand reach, and denser service networks.

**Why it matters:** The combination would materially reshape independent retail coverage, but the network and revenue effects depend on closing, integration, and retention of local customers and staff.

*Published 2026-07-15 | Nordic Tyre Group; Dinamic 92 Distribution*

## Sales & Customer Services

### Currency swings overwhelm Linglong's first-half operating improvement

*Published 2026-07-14 | Linglong Tire*

**What changed:** Linglong preliminarily estimated first-half attributable net profit of CNY 110 million, down 87 percent, and adjusted net profit of CNY 9 million, down 99 percent. It attributed the decline mainly to a swing from CNY 691 million of foreign-exchange gains a year earlier to about CNY 342 million of losses, while saying operations improved before foreign exchange.

**Intelligence signal:** For globally expanding Chinese tire makers, currency management can now outweigh plant and sales execution in reported earnings.

**Why it matters:** The roughly CNY 1.03 billion year-on-year foreign-exchange swing shows why investors and operators need to separate operating progress from translation and settlement effects.

**Sources:** [Linglong Tire filing mirror; Eastmoney](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12444790&stockid=601966) | [second source](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607143805650148.html)

### Goodyear adds 66 winter-tire fitments, mostly for 19-inch-and-larger wheels

*Published 2026-07-14 | Goodyear*

**What changed:** Goodyear expanded the UltraGrip Performance 3 range by 66 stock-keeping units to 344, with more than 90 percent of the additions sized 19 inches or larger. The expansion includes 59 replacement-market sizes and seven new original-equipment fitments for the BMW iX3, Porsche Cayenne Electric, and Mercedes-Benz GLC EQ.

**Intelligence signal:** Winter-tire range growth is shifting toward larger wheel diameters and electric-vehicle applications while one tread family spans both replacement and OE demand.

**Why it matters:** Broader fitment coverage can improve dealer availability and capture higher-value vehicle applications, although stock-keeping-unit breadth does not by itself demonstrate sales or profitability.

**Sources:** [Goodyear](https://news.goodyear.eu/goodyear-ultragrip-performance-3--one-tread-pattern-delivering-the-broadest-winter-tire-coverage-on-the-market)

### Japan's tire-demand revision pairs a stronger first half with a sharp second-half slowdown

*Published 2026-07-14 | Japan Automobile Tyre Manufacturers Association*

**What changed:** JATMA revised its 2026 domestic automotive-tire demand forecast to 103.67 million units, down 2% from 2025. The association recorded first-half demand 3% higher but assumes a 5% second-half decline; four-wheel original-equipment demand is forecast flat at 37.05 million units while replacement manufacturer shipments fall 2% to 64.48 million.

**Intelligence signal:** Japan's market signal is a split-year pattern: early gains are not carrying into the second-half outlook, and replacement weakness is broader than original-equipment demand.

**Why it matters:** The revision provides a source-owned planning baseline for production, inventory, and channel decisions, with truck-and-bus replacement shipments forecast down 3% even as truck-and-bus OE demand rises 2%.

**Sources:** [Japan Automobile Tyre Manufacturers Association](https://www.jatma.or.jp/toukei/download.php?id=anV5b3UvMjAyNl9taW5hb3NoaS5wZGY%3D) | [second source](https://www.jatma.or.jp/toukei/list.php?cat_id=anV5b3U%3D)

### Volume, pricing and lower factory costs lift Nokian's profitability

*Published 2026-07-17 | Nokian Tyres*

**What changed:** Nokian Tyres reported second-quarter net sales of EUR 379.9 million, up 10.6 percent, and operating profit of EUR 34.8 million versus EUR 14.8 million a year earlier. It attributed the profitability improvement to higher sales volumes, enhanced pricing, and lower manufacturing and material costs.

**Intelligence signal:** Commercial recovery is being supported by a combination of volume, pricing discipline, and factory-cost normalization rather than demand growth alone.

**Why it matters:** The result shows improving earnings leverage during Nokian's capacity transition, but negative quarterly operating cash flow and uncertain demand temper the headline profit gain.

**Sources:** [Nokian Tyres](https://company.nokiantyres.com/news-and-media/news-article/nokian-tyres-plc-half-year-financial-report-january-june-2026-strong-profitability-improvement-driven-by-higher-sales-volumes-and-enhanced-pricing)

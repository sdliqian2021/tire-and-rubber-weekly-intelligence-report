## Manufacturing

### China proposes tighter and broader air-emission controls for tire plants

*Published 2026-07-13 | China Ministry of Ecology and Environment*

**What changed:** China's environment ministry opened consultation on a draft national rubber-products air-pollutant emission standard. The proposal broadens process and pollutant coverage, explicitly encompasses tire manufacturing, tightens organized-emission limits, and adds fugitive-emission and monitoring requirements.

**Intelligence signal:** Environmental control is moving from narrow end-of-pipe limits toward plant-wide process, fugitive-emission, and monitoring obligations.

**Why it matters:** If adopted, the standard could require tire plants to reassess capture systems, treatment capacity, monitoring points, operating records, and capital plans.

**Sources:** [China Ministry of Ecology and Environment; China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk06/202607/t20260713_1161579.html) | [second source](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2076859217608806401)

### Rizhao clears Haomai's green curing-equipment project at the EIA stage

*Published 2026-07-16 | Haomai Machinery Technology (Rizhao)*

**What changed:** Rizhao Development Zone's ecological-environment branch approved the environmental-impact assessment for Haomai's high-end tire green curing-equipment and key-parts industrialization project. Public reporting gives a full-project target of 20,000 mold-shell units and 6,000 key components per year.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-equipment suppliers are pairing curing-efficiency upgrades with dedicated industrial-scale component capacity.

**Why it matters:** The project could strengthen domestic supply of curing hardware, but its commercial effect depends on construction, equipment installation, qualification, and customer demand after permitting.

**Sources:** [Sina Finance; China Natural Rubber Association](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-07-16/doc-inihzmmw1423833.shtml) | [second source](https://www.cnraw.org.cn/index.php/article/jingyanjiaoliu/20938.html)


## R&D and Technology

### Backbone-rigid functionalization makes natural rubber stronger and mechanically deconstructable

*Published 2026-07-16 | Shanghai Jiao Tong University*

**What changed:** Researchers reported a catalyst- and additive-free photochemical route that introduces polar rigid bicyclic cyclobutane units into the natural-rubber backbone. Functionalization reached up to 28 mol percent in ten minutes at room temperature; a tested vulcanized formulation showed roughly fivefold gains in strength and toughness, while mechanophore opening enabled force-triggered deconstruction.

**Intelligence signal:** Polymer design is combining high performance with programmed end-of-life behavior directly in the natural-rubber backbone.

**Why it matters:** The chemistry points toward elastomers that can be tuned across flow, elastic, and plastic regimes while retaining a route to mechanical deconstruction, a potentially important circularity lever.

**Sources:** [Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Angewandte Chemie International Edition](https://news.sjtu.edu.cn/jdzh/20260716/224704.html) | [second source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/anie.1326350)


## Supply Chain

### July reference prices expose wide regional spreads in tire chemicals

*Published 2026-07-14 | China Rubber Industry Association*

**What changed:** The association's July reference table showed substantial cross-city ranges for tire inputs, including sulfur at CNY 3,500 to 11,000 per tonne, insoluble sulfur at CNY 7,800 to 26,000, butadiene rubber at CNY 12,500 to 14,000, and SBR 1502 at CNY 12,500 to 14,200.

**Intelligence signal:** Regional procurement conditions and specification differences can dominate a single national price narrative for tire inputs.

**Why it matters:** The spreads reinforce the need to benchmark by grade, delivery market, and supplier rather than using one headline number for compound-cost planning.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://cria.org.cn/a/2076944137060454402)


## Marketing and Sales

### Currency swings overwhelm Linglong's first-half operating improvement

*Published 2026-07-14 | Linglong Tire*

**What changed:** Linglong preliminarily estimated first-half attributable net profit of CNY 110 million, down 87 percent, and adjusted net profit of CNY 9 million, down 99 percent. It attributed the decline mainly to a swing from CNY 691 million of foreign-exchange gains a year earlier to about CNY 342 million of losses, while saying operations improved before foreign exchange.

**Intelligence signal:** For globally expanding Chinese tire makers, currency management can now outweigh plant and sales execution in reported earnings.

**Why it matters:** The roughly CNY 1.03 billion year-on-year foreign-exchange swing shows why investors and operators need to separate operating progress from translation and settlement effects.

**Sources:** [Linglong Tire filing mirror; Eastmoney](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12444790&stockid=601966) | [second source](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607143805650148.html)

### China's tire exports grow in volume through June

*Published 2026-07-19 | Chinese tire exporters*

**What changed:** A trade-data report citing China Customs said first-half tire exports reached 4.94 million tonnes, up 4.9 percent year on year, with export value of CNY 82.6 billion.

**Intelligence signal:** Export tonnage is still growing even as price, mix, trade-remedy, and overseas-localization pressures complicate the value story.

**Why it matters:** The volume gain keeps external demand central to Chinese tire utilization, but tonnage alone cannot show whether margins, units, or product mix improved.

**Sources:** [QinRex via Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/future/wemedia/2026-07-19/doc-iniikhxa4922175.shtml)

## R&D & Product Development

### Techking and Breton link tire data to an autonomous-mine control model

*Published 2026-07-22 | Techking Tires; Breton Technology*

**What changed:** Techking and autonomous-mining specialist Breton expanded cooperation covering electric mining-truck tires, joint R&D, smart-mine data, and lifecycle service. The companies plan to feed tire parameters into Breton's autonomous-driving model and combine vehicle, tire, road, and cloud data for monitoring and fleet optimization.

**Intelligence signal:** Industrial tires are becoming sensed and modeled assets inside autonomous fleet-control systems rather than standalone consumables.

**Why it matters:** Tire-condition and performance data can improve dispatch, energy use, maintenance timing, and safety in high-load mining operations if the integration works in production fleets.

**Sources:** [First Construction Machinery Network](https://news.d1cm.com/20260722190796.shtml)

### Linglong and Dawn move DVA innerliner material into small-batch production

*Published 2026-07-23 | Linglong Tire; Dawn Polymer*

**What changed:** Linglong disclosed that a dynamically vulcanized alloy gas-barrier material entered small-batch production after work on adhesion, online film application, and a 2025 batch road test. The partners report gas impermeability seven to ten times that of a traditional innerliner material, with potential weight and rolling-resistance benefits.

**Intelligence signal:** Thermoplastic-elastomer barrier films are moving from laboratory development toward manufacturable tire innerliners.

**Why it matters:** If durability, adhesion, process stability, and economics hold at scale, DVA could reduce innerliner mass and support lower rolling resistance without abandoning conventional tire architectures.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2080173687517057025)

## Materials & Supply Chain

### Longxing moves tire-pyrolysis-oil carbon black into European batch supply

*Published 2026-07-24 | Longxing Chemical*

**What changed:** Longxing said selected sustainable-carbon-black grades made with tire-pyrolysis oil passed certification at an unnamed large European tire maker and entered batch supply, with a first order near 10,000 tonnes. The company began the program in 2024 and completed environmental assessment work for the product line in late 2025.

**Intelligence signal:** Recovered tire-pyrolysis oil is being used as carbon-black feedstock in certified tire supply, creating a distinct circular pathway from directly recovered carbon black.

**Why it matters:** A verified repeatable supply route could scale circular carbon content through existing carbon-black manufacturing and qualification systems.

**Sources:** [Longxing Chemical announcement via Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-07-24/doc-iniivpqz1973319.shtml)

## Manufacturing

### Prinx turns its Kedah factory plan toward site works and local supply-chain execution

*Published 2026-07-23 | Prinx Chengshan*

**What changed:** Prinx reported discussions with Kedah officials on its Malaysia factory, local supply-chain coordination, electricity, and approvals, then opened a tender for first-stage landscaping work at the Kedah Rubber City site.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire projects in Southeast Asia are shifting from investment announcements into local permitting, utilities, supplier integration, and early site packages.

**Why it matters:** The combination provides a concrete execution signal for the Malaysia project and shows which local dependencies must be resolved before manufacturing can begin.

**Sources:** [Prinx Chengshan](https://www.prinxchengshan.com/index.php?id=6509) | [second source](https://www.prinxchengshan.com/index.php?id=6511)

### Yuanxing opens building tenders for its first overseas tire factory in Indonesia

*Published 2026-07-23 | Sichuan Yuanxing Rubber*

**What changed:** Public reporting said Yuanxing opened building-work tenders for a phased tire-factory project at the Batang Integrated Industrial Estate in Central Java, Indonesia. The reported site covers about 405,000 square metres and would be the company's first overseas tire factory.

**Intelligence signal:** Mid-sized Chinese tire manufacturers are joining the overseas-capacity wave through Indonesian industrial estates and phased procurement.

**Why it matters:** Tendering is an early but tangible step toward diversifying production geography and reducing reliance on direct exports from China.

**Sources:** [Tire Business via Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-07-23/doc-iniiufve8311240.shtml)

## Sales & Customer Services

### Prinx pairs Thailand-made OE fitments with local-rubber sourcing

*Published 2026-07-24 | Prinx Chengshan*

**What changed:** Prinx said its Thailand-made Aquila Pro tires secured fitments on the MG5 PRO, MG S5 EV, and Changan NEVO Q05 in Thailand. It also signed a memorandum with an unnamed upstream partner to strengthen local natural-rubber sourcing.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese-owned overseas tire plants are combining local vehicle fitment wins with host-country raw-material integration.

**Why it matters:** The pairing can improve plant utilization, shorten supply chains, and embed the Thailand operation more deeply in regional automotive and rubber ecosystems.

**Sources:** [Prinx Chengshan](https://www.prinxchengshan.com/index.php?id=6512)

## R&D & Product Development

### Techking and Breton link tire data to an autonomous-mine control model

*Published 2026-07-22 | Techking Tires; Breton Technology*

**What changed:** Techking and autonomous-mining specialist Breton expanded cooperation covering electric mining-truck tires, joint R&D, smart-mine data, and lifecycle service. The companies plan to feed tire parameters into Breton's autonomous-driving model and combine vehicle, tire, road, and cloud data for monitoring and fleet optimization.

**Intelligence signal:** Industrial tires are becoming sensed and modeled assets inside autonomous fleet-control systems rather than standalone consumables.

**Why it matters:** Tire-condition and performance data can improve dispatch, energy use, maintenance timing, and safety in high-load mining operations if the integration works in production fleets.

**Sources:** [First Construction Machinery Network](https://news.d1cm.com/20260722190796.shtml)

### Linglong and Dawn move DVA innerliner material into small-batch production

*Published 2026-07-23 | Linglong Tire; Dawn Polymer*

**What changed:** Linglong disclosed that a dynamically vulcanized alloy gas-barrier material entered small-batch production after work on adhesion, online film application, and a 2025 batch road test. The partners report gas impermeability seven to ten times that of a traditional innerliner material, with potential weight and rolling-resistance benefits.

**Intelligence signal:** Thermoplastic-elastomer barrier films are moving from laboratory development toward manufacturable tire innerliners.

**Why it matters:** If durability, adhesion, process stability, and economics hold at scale, DVA could reduce innerliner mass and support lower rolling resistance without abandoning conventional tire architectures.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2080173687517057025)

### Bridgestone launches Potenza Sport Evo in Thailand with EV-ready construction

*Published 2026-07-20 | Bridgestone*

**What changed:** Bridgestone Thailand launched the Potenza Sport Evo for high-performance cars and EVs. It lists 71 sizes from 18 to 22 inches for 2026 and says 37 became available in July, with the remainder planned for the fourth quarter.

**Intelligence signal:** The design combines curved tread blocks, differentiated grooves, nylon-and-aramid reinforcement, and a lightweight construction intended to balance grip, wear, and EV efficiency.

**Why it matters:** A broad size rollout in an Asian replacement market shows premium manufacturers adapting established ultra-high-performance architectures for heavier, torque-rich EVs rather than treating EV tires as a separate niche.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone Thailand](https://www.bridgestone.co.th/en/media-centre/press-release/2026/bridgestone-introduces-new-bridgestone-potenza-sport-evo)


## Materials & Supply Chain

### Pirelli, Pyrum, BASF, and Synthos assemble a European tire-to-tire material loop

*Published 2026-07-22 | Pirelli; Pyrum Innovations; BASF; Synthos*

**What changed:** The partners launched a chain that collects end-of-life and factory-scrap tires in Germany, converts them into recovered carbon black and tire-pyrolysis oil, upgrades the carbon black for Pirelli, and uses the oil through BASF and Synthos mass-balance routes to make certified synthetic rubber for new Pirelli tires.

**Intelligence signal:** Circular tire programs are moving from isolated recycling trials toward coordinated, traceable multi-company systems spanning collection, pyrolysis, chemicals, polymers, and tire production.

**Why it matters:** The project creates an industrial route for replacing two important virgin inputs and, if volumes and economics scale, could become a template for closed-loop procurement rather than downcycling.

**Sources:** [Pirelli; Deutsche Boerse/Pyrum](https://press.pirelli.com/pirelli-pyrum-synthos-and-basf-partner-to-transform-endoflife-and-scrap-tyres-into-recycled-materials-for-new-products) | [second source](https://live.deutsche-boerse.com/news/EQS-News-Pyrum-signs-long-term-supply-and-offtake-agreements-with-Pirelli-as-part-of-the-European-Tyre-to-Tyre-initiative-7ec3f560-4b17-4845-ab16-c3f5cdcabe90)

### Longxing moves tire-pyrolysis-oil carbon black into European batch supply

*Published 2026-07-24 | Longxing Chemical*

**What changed:** Longxing said selected sustainable-carbon-black grades made with tire-pyrolysis oil passed certification at an unnamed large European tire maker and entered batch supply, with a first order near 10,000 tonnes. The company began the program in 2024 and completed environmental assessment work for the product line in late 2025.

**Intelligence signal:** Recovered tire-pyrolysis oil is being used as carbon-black feedstock in certified tire supply, creating a distinct circular pathway from directly recovered carbon black.

**Why it matters:** If customer confirmation and repeat deliveries follow, this route could scale circular carbon content through existing carbon-black manufacturing and qualification systems.

**Sources:** [Longxing Chemical announcement via Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-07-24/doc-iniivpqz1973319.shtml)

### Binhua approves a phased 70,000-tonne halobutyl-rubber project in Shandong

*Published 2026-07-20 | Binhua Group; Shandong Binhua New Material*

**What changed:** Binhua disclosed a planned CNY 1.82 billion, two-phase project in Binzhou for 70,000 tonnes per year of chlorobutyl and bromobutyl rubber, with the first phase scheduled to start construction in 2027.

**Intelligence signal:** Shandong's tire cluster is adding domestic high-barrier elastomer capacity and integrating it with local C4 feedstock and planned green power.

**Why it matters:** The project could reduce import exposure for tire innerliner materials, but it remains an investment plan rather than built or qualified capacity.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association citing Binhua announcement](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2079112967698354177)

### German rubber producers report better availability but no raw-material cost relief

*Published 2026-07-22 | wdk members; German rubber industry*

**What changed:** A third-quarter survey by Germany's rubber-industry association found that immediate shortage concerns had eased, but raw-material prices remained high. Its index for a typical natural-rubber mix reached the highest level in about two years, while export business was more positive than domestic demand.

**Intelligence signal:** The supply-chain problem is shifting from physical availability toward sustained input-cost pressure, energy exposure, and weak domestic demand.

**Why it matters:** This distinction matters for tire and technical-rubber margins: materials may be obtainable without being affordable, increasing pressure for pricing, formulation changes, sourcing diversification, and efficiency gains.

**Sources:** [wdk - German Rubber Industry Association](https://wdk.de/pressemitteilungen/kautschukindustrie-rohstoffe-verfuegbar-kosten-bleiben-hoch) | [second source](https://reifenpresse.de/2026/07/22/keine-rohstoffengpaesse-in-der-kautschukbranche-aber-weiter-hohe-kosten)

### Michelin ResiCare appoints IMCD to distribute French-made bio-based 5-HMF

*Published 2026-07-23 | Michelin ResiCare; IMCD*

**What changed:** Michelin ResiCare signed IMCD Europe to distribute 5-hydroxymethylfurfural across Europe from 2027, adding logistics and local technical support beyond direct strategic accounts. Michelin says its Peage-de-Roussillon unit is scheduled to start in early 2027 with 3,000 tonnes of annual capacity.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire-company polymer expertise is expanding upstream into renewable chemical intermediates and technical distribution models for multiple industries.

**Why it matters:** 5-HMF can serve as an input for lower-impact resins and other formulations, while the distribution agreement tests whether Michelin can commercialize materials know-how beyond tires at industrial scale.

**Sources:** [Michelin; Michelin ResiCare](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/partnership-bio-sourced-molecule) | [second source](https://resicare.michelin.com/news/michelin-resicare-axens-bio-based-5-hmf)

## Manufacturing

### Goodyear files plan to close Fayetteville and remove 1,750 jobs

*Published 2026-07-21 | Goodyear*

**What changed:** Goodyear told the US Securities and Exchange Commission that it agreed with the United Steelworkers and approved permanent closure of its Fayetteville, North Carolina plant. The plan covers about 1,750 job reductions, is expected to be substantially complete by the end of 2027, and carries estimated pretax charges of $535 million to $565 million.

**Intelligence signal:** Western tire manufacturing is undergoing capacity rationalization aimed at lowering regional cost per tire rather than adding volume.

**Why it matters:** The closure is a material North American footprint decision. Goodyear forecasts about $270 million in annual Americas operating-income improvement from 2028, but it also removes skilled jobs and concentrates production elsewhere.

**Sources:** [US SEC; Goodyear filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/42582/000162828026049010/gt-20260716.htm) | [second source](https://www.secinfo.com/d1HM3n.N1frt.htm)

### Weifang Jinyuan proposes electric curing in a 600,000-unit OTR tire retrofit

*Published 2026-07-22 | Weifang Jinyuan Rubber & Plastic*

**What changed:** An EIA pre-filing disclosure described a CNY 110 million retrofit that would replace two mixers and shift curing from steam to electric heating while keeping annual OTR tire capacity at 600,000 units.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire-plant decarbonization is moving toward electrified curing and equipment replacement without requiring headline capacity expansion.

**Why it matters:** The project tests whether existing plants can reduce process-energy emissions and improve control while holding output constant.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association citing EIA disclosure](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2079834278716346369)

### CEAT's annual report shifts attention to Camso integration and global off-highway execution

*Published 2026-07-23 | CEAT; Camso*

**What changed:** CEAT posted its FY2025-26 integrated annual report and described Camso integration as a central execution program across products, markets, channels, OEM customers, manufacturing, and customer engagement. The report lists consolidated revenue of INR156.78 billion, nearly 19 percent above the prior year, while flagging input-cost inflation and supply disruption for FY2026-27.

**Intelligence signal:** Indian tire makers are using acquisitions and integrated manufacturing-channel programs to move into higher-value global off-highway segments.

**Why it matters:** Camso gives CEAT a wider technology and customer base, but realizing the value depends on manufacturing integration, cross-selling, and cost control during a period of raw-material inflation.

**Sources:** [CEAT BSE annual-report filing](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/0f1af1bc-e27b-4adb-aa5d-2560edd43ba6.pdf)

### CRIA translates China's draft rubber-emission standard into plant-level compliance priorities

*Published 2026-07-24 | China Rubber Industry Association; Chinese tire and rubber plants*

**What changed:** CRIA's in-window technical interpretation detailed proposed controls covering tire manufacturing, including new carbon-disulfide and combustion limits, tighter particulate limits, plant-level fugitive VOC monitoring, and added collection requirements for cooling, warehouses, and wastewater treatment.

**Intelligence signal:** China's environmental rulemaking is shifting from a general consultation signal toward specific plant-wide monitoring and capture obligations.

**Why it matters:** The interpretation gives tire plants a concrete checklist for capital planning and comments, while the underlying standard remains a draft that may change.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2080550719434362881) | [second source](https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk06/202607/t20260713_1161579.html)


## Marketing & Distribution

### Germany fines Maxxis and wholesalers EUR 11.9 million over resale-price restrictions

*Published 2026-07-20 | Bundeskartellamt; Maxxis International; Best4Tires Berlin; Reifen Mueller*

**What changed:** Germany's Bundeskartellamt imposed fines totaling EUR 11.9 million on Maxxis International, Best4Tires Berlin, Reifen Mueller, and one responsible individual. The authority said margin-guarantee agreements restricted dealer pricing for Maxxis and CST tires, particularly on the Tyre24 platform, while Maxxis monitored selling prices and intervened when it considered them too low.

**Intelligence signal:** Online price transparency is increasing both the commercial temptation and competition-law risk around resale-price monitoring in tire wholesale channels.

**Why it matters:** Tire makers, importers, and distributors should audit margin support, recommended-price communications, and marketplace-monitoring practices while preserving dealers' independent pricing.

**Sources:** [Bundeskartellamt; Gummibereifung](https://www.bundeskartellamt.de/SharedDocs/Meldung/EN/Pressemitteilungen/2026/07_20_26_Maxxis_CST.html) | [second source](https://www.gummibereifung.de/preisabsprachen-im-reifengeschaeft-119-mio-euro-bussgeld-gegen-maxxis-international-best4tires)

### Mavis agrees to buy Pep Boys for $700 million and expand westward

*Published 2026-07-21 | Mavis Tire Express Services; Pep Boys; Icahn Enterprises*

**What changed:** Mavis agreed to acquire Pep Boys for about $700 million in cash, subject to adjustments and closing conditions. Pep Boys operates nearly 800 service locations; Mavis says the deal would take its network above 4,400 locations across the United States and Canada, while Icahn retains specified real estate, AAMCO, and Precision Tune Auto Care.

**Intelligence signal:** North American tire retail and automotive service are consolidating into larger omnichannel networks with broader procurement, distribution, fitting, and customer reach.

**Why it matters:** The transaction could change tire-brand access and bargaining power across the replacement channel, especially in the western United States, but effects depend on closing and post-deal integration.

**Sources:** [Mavis; Icahn Enterprises](https://www.mavis.com/news/mavis-pep-boys) | [second source](https://www.ielp.com/node/5816)

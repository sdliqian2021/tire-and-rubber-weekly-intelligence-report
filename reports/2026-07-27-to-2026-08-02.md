## R&D & Product Development

### General Science says its 6PPD-free OTR compound entered mass production

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-29 | General Science; Otsuka Materials; Qingdao University; Dazhan Nano*

**What changed:** CRIA reported that General Science launched an OTR tire compound the company says fully replaces 6PPD, improves durability by more than 10 percent, and greatly reduces aquatic toxicity. General Science reported a 505/95R29 XR992 mine-road test and said the product entered mass production.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire makers are moving 6PPD-alternative development from passenger-tire research into severe-service OTR applications.

**Why it matters:** A scalable alternative that survives mining duty could materially accelerate antidegradant substitution, but comparative safety and durability must be independently verified.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2082334511136346113)

### Isuzu makes SRI wheel-detachment warning standard on the new Erga

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | Sumitomo Rubber Industries; Isuzu Motors*

**What changed:** Sumitomo Rubber Industries said the wheel-detachment early-warning function of SENSING CORE became standard equipment on Isuzu's updated Erga large route and shuttle bus, which Isuzu released for sale on July 27. The system analyzes wheel-speed and vehicle CAN data to flag abnormal wheel-nut loosening.

**Intelligence signal:** Tire and wheel intelligence is moving from demonstrations into standard OEM integration on commercial vehicles, using vehicle data to detect maintenance-related risk.

**Why it matters:** Standard fitment creates a concrete fleet deployment path for tire-derived safety analytics, but it does not yet establish real-world incident reduction.

**Sources:** [Sumitomo Rubber Industries / Isuzu Motors](https://www.srigroup.co.jp/newsrelease/2026/sri/2026_075.html) | [second source](https://www.isuzu-global.com/ja/newsroom/20260727_1.html)

### Bridgestone sets a September launch for BLIZZAK ICEPEAK with higher-load winter performance claims

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | Bridgestone*

**What changed:** Bridgestone announced that BLIZZAK ICEPEAK will go on sale in Japan on September 1 in 57 sizes for SUVs, CUVs, EVs, hybrids, and other high-load or high-torque vehicles. In disclosed company comparisons with BLIZZAK DM-V3, it reported an 11 percent shorter ice-braking distance and 14 percent longer wear life, plus greater modeled edge effect in snow.

**Intelligence signal:** Winter-tire development is adapting grip, wear, and load capability together for larger and electrified vehicles rather than treating EV suitability as a separate niche.

**Why it matters:** A 57-size rollout by a major manufacturer indicates broad commercialization, and simultaneously improving ice braking and wear could reduce a traditional winter-tire tradeoff; however, sales had not begun during the reporting week and the performance evidence is manufacturer-generated.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone Corporation](https://www.bridgestone.co.jp/corporate/news/2026072701.html)

### Nano-silica strengthens natural-rubber tire sealant in simulated puncture tests

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | Tsinghua University; Zhejiang Liming Fortune Technical Co., Ltd.; Zhejiang University of Technology*

**What changed:** Researchers dispersed hydrophobic nano-silica in natural-rubber latex using SDBS and ultrasonication; in simulated puncture testing, the authors report a roughly 3 km shorter critical repair distance and more than 75 percent less pressure loss than the reference formulation.

**Intelligence signal:** Latex-stage nano-silica dispersion for faster, lower-leakage self-sealing tire compounds.

**Why it matters:** The result points to a potentially more reliable puncture-sealant formulation, but it remains laboratory evidence rather than road or fleet validation.

**Sources:** [Chemistry (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-8549/8/8/103)

### Microwave drying and TDH cut a rolling-resistance proxy in natural rubber

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-30 | Qingdao University of Science and Technology; National Engineering Laboratory of Advanced Tire Equipment and Key Materials; Hubei Institute of Aerospace Chemotechnology*

**What changed:** A Chinese team combined terephthalic dihydrazide interfacial modification with microwave drying in natural-rubber compounds. At 0.75 phr TDH, tan delta at 60 degrees C fell 46.53 percent, tensile strength rose 17.3 percent, and abrasion volume fell 22.6 percent versus the control.

**Intelligence signal:** Coupled chemical-interface modification and microwave processing for lower-hysteresis, better-dispersed filled rubber.

**Why it matters:** The combination could improve the rolling-resistance, strength, and wear balance sought in tires, but tan delta is a laboratory proxy rather than direct tire performance.

**Sources:** [Journal of Applied Polymer Science](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/app.71264)

### High-molecular-weight plasticizer slows antioxidant migration in NR/BR

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | Beijing University of Chemical Technology; State Key Laboratory of Organic-Inorganic Composites*

**What changed:** Researchers partially replaced TDAE oil with polyester polyol D1050 in an NR/BR compound. At 7 phr D1050, antioxidant 4020 migration fell 21.27 percent and the aging coefficient rose 18.5 percent, with mechanical properties reported as essentially unchanged.

**Intelligence signal:** High-molecular-weight plasticization to retain antidegradant within rubber and reduce surface bloom.

**Why it matters:** Slower antioxidant loss may extend ozone-aging protection and improve appearance, but the result has not been validated in tires or long-duration service.

**Sources:** [Advanced Engineering Materials](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adem.202600012)

## Materials & Supply Chain

### China input-price board shows divergent costs while benchmark tire quotes hold flat

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | China rubber and tire supply chain*

**What changed:** Across consecutive CRIA and Longzhong weekly boards, the reported average reference value for antidegradant 4020 rose from 17,660 to 18,700 and butadiene from 10,365 to 10,840, while N330 carbon black fell from 7,460 to 7,300. The listed 205/55R16 passenger-tire and 12R22.5 truck-tire references remained at 198 and 850.

**Intelligence signal:** Selected tire inputs moved in different directions while two benchmark finished-tire quotes did not change week to week.

**Why it matters:** The divergence points to possible margin pressure if chemical costs rise faster than tire quotes; however, the boards do not state units, sampling, transaction basis, or weighting and therefore show directional reference movement rather than realized market prices.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association / Longzhong Information](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2081530009839833089) | [second source](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2078993294411534338)

### Surface modification raises recycled carbon black adhesion in natural rubber

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-27 | Qingdao University of Science and Technology; South China University of Technology; Shuangqian Group (Jiangsu) Tire Co., Ltd.*

**What changed:** Researchers treated end-of-life-tire pyrolysis carbon black with three surface modifiers before blending it into natural rubber. The best-balanced 10 wt percent pentaerythritol tetraacrylate treatment improved self-adhesion by 224 percent, while diethyl maleate was identified as the more economical option.

**Intelligence signal:** Chemical surface treatment to upgrade recycled carbon black performance in natural-rubber compounds.

**Why it matters:** Better dispersion and adhesion could increase recycled-carbon-black feasibility in tire compounds, although finished-tire performance and economics remain unproven.

**Sources:** [Journal of Applied Polymer Science](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/app.71258)

### Twin-screw desulfurization improves crumb-rubber asphalt rheology

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-29 | Gansu Highway Traffic Construction Group; Lanzhou Jiaotong University; Gansu Transportation Planning Survey & Design Institute; Lanzhou University of Technology*

**What changed:** Researchers used twin-screw extrusion to produce desulfurized rubber powders at different solubilities and blended them with three asphalt binders. The modified powders improved compatibility, while the Shell 90# system showed the strongest high-temperature rutting and permanent-deformation resistance.

**Intelligence signal:** Controlled twin-screw desulfurization of waste-tire rubber for more compatible asphalt modifiers.

**Why it matters:** The process offers another value route for end-of-life tire rubber, but binder-scale rheology does not establish field pavement life or environmental performance.

**Sources:** [Materials (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/19/15/3227)

### Monro holds tire units flat as customers trade down

### Michelin lifts H1 operating income and cash flow despite OE weakness

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span></p>

### CRIA chair warns China's tire output-value growth is not restoring profit

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-29 | China Rubber Industry Association member companies*

**What changed:** In a July 29 interview, the association chair said data from 389 member companies for January through May showed gross output value up 4.39 percent while profit fell 8.97 percent. Tire export delivery value fell 4.3 percent, including 8.5 percent in May, even though the sector remained heavily export dependent.

**Intelligence signal:** China's tire challenge is shifting from chasing scale to restoring price discipline and profit quality amid capacity, trade, procurement, and online-pricing pressure.

**Why it matters:** The divergence between current-price output value and profit indicates that higher reported output value alone will not resolve low-price competition or improve returns on new capacity.

**Sources:** [China Rubber Industry Association](https://www.cria.org.cn/a/2082336539988299778)

### EU commercial-vehicle registrations rebound as electrified fleets gain share

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span></p>

*Published 2026-07-29 | European Automobile Manufacturers' Association*

**What changed:** ACEA reported that first-half EU registrations rose 1.9 percent to 742,759 vans, 9.8 percent to 171,933 trucks, and 22.7 percent to 22,590 buses. Electrically chargeable registrations grew 41.6 percent for vans, 47.7 percent for trucks, and 56.8 percent for buses, while diesel still held 92.1 percent of the truck market.

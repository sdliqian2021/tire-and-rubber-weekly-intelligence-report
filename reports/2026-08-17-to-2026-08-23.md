### AFM and NMR separate crosslink and entanglement changes in stretched vulcanized butadiene rubber

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-08-21 | Industrial Technology Center of Okayama Prefecture; Institute of Science Tokyo*

Researchers combined atomic-force-microscopy nanomechanics, time-domain NMR, tensile tests, and a tube model to study vulcanized butadiene rubber under elongation. The fitted BR modulus split into crosslink and entanglement contributions of 0.21 and 0.54 MPa, and NMR indicated fewer constrained components up to an elongation ratio of 3.5. The method provides local structure-property information that bulk tensile testing alone cannot show and could improve formulation models. Evidence is from a simple unfilled BR compound and does not yet cover production tire compounds. Watch for Application to silica- and carbon-black-filled tread compounds, cyclic fatigue, temperature sweeps, and links to tire-level durability or hysteresis.

**Sources:** [Polymer Journal](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41428-026-01250-w)

### UV-aged tire-wear particles cut tomato shoot biomass 77.1% in a high-dose soil test

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-08-20 | Nankai University and partners*

A 30-day tomato experiment applied UV-aged tire-wear particles of 100 or 200 micrometers at 0.1% or 1% soil concentration. The 1% 100-micrometer condition reduced shoot biomass 77.1% and net assimilation 62.1%, increased malondialdehyde 72%, and altered soil enzymes and rhizosphere communities. The paper isolates particle size and photoaging as factors that can change tire-wear environmental effects. The high-dose pot experiment is hazard evidence, not a field exposure or human-risk estimate. Watch for Field-realistic concentrations, multiple soils and crops, chemical-source apportionment, and measured environmental exposure distributions.

**Sources:** [Environmental Pollution](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0269749126013722)

### Linglong moves DVA innerliner material into stable small-batch tire production

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-01 | Linglong Tire*

Linglong said its DVA program had reached about 5,000 trial tires per month after it and Dawn developed a co-continuous crosslinked structure and an online film-application process. A high-barrier thermoplastic-elastomer innerliner is moving from material promise toward process-compatible tire production. The stated batch rate is concrete industrial evidence, but the 7-10 times barrier, 80% weight and 50% durability claims are company figures without a disclosed test protocol. Watch for homologation, named vehicle trials, scrap rates and independently tested gas retention and durability.

**Sources:** [China Tire Business](https://www.tirechina.net/833004.html)

*Published 2026-08-31 | Guizhou Tyre*

Guizhou Tyre introduced metric and light-truck products for urban delivery, citing reinforced carcasses, low-heat compounds and wet-grip features for heavier, frequently stopping electric vehicles. The products translate electric-truck duty cycles into explicit construction and compound choices, although the source gives no controlled comparative test values. Look for fleet mileage, rolling-resistance and wet-braking results against named conventional products.

### Toyo launches Proxes G-Tour for gas, hybrid and electric vehicles

*Published 2026-09-01 | Toyo Tire*

Toyo introduced the Proxes G-Tour grand-touring all-season replacement tire, using an asymmetric tread and five-variable pitch design and beginning initial US sales in September. ### China natural-rubber spot assessment reaches RMB17,900 per tonne

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-04 | Business Society / SunSirs*

A Chinese market report placed a natural-rubber spot assessment at RMB17,900 per tonne on 4 September, about 14% higher year to date, alongside a new round of tire price letters. Natural-rubber strength is arriving together with carbon-black and synthetic-rubber pressure, narrowing manufacturers' ability to offset one input with another. A single assessed price is not a transaction-weighted industry average and grades, locations and contract timing vary. Compare exchange settlements, physical premiums and tire-company procurement disclosures.

**Sources:** [Sina Finance / Business Society](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-09-04/doc-iniqrpra1468555.shtml)

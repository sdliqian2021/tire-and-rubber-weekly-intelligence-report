### China natural-rubber spot assessment reaches RMB17,900 per tonne

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-04 | Business Society / SunSirs*

**What changed:** A Chinese market report placed a natural-rubber spot assessment at RMB17,900 per tonne on 4 September, about 14% higher year to date, alongside a new round of tire price letters.

**Why it matters:** A single assessed price is not a transaction-weighted industry average and grades, locations and contract timing vary.

**Intelligence signal:** Natural-rubber strength is arriving together with carbon-black and synthetic-rubber pressure, narrowing manufacturers' ability to offset one input with another.

**Sources:** [Sina Finance / Business Society](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-09-04/doc-iniqrpra1468555.shtml)

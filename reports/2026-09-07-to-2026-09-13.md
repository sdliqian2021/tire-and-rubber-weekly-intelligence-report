### Nokian launches Hakkapeliitta 01 with temperature-responsive studs

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-10 | Nokian Tyres*

Nokian introduced an adaptive base layer that firms in ice and snow to push studs outward and softens on warmer pavement so they retract deeper into the tread. Mechanical winter grip is becoming condition-responsive at the material-structure interface rather than fixed for all surfaces. Nokian claims up to 30% less road wear, 1 dB less cabin noise, 10% better ice grip and 5% better wet grip versus Hakkapeliitta 10; protocols were not supplied. Look for independent winter tests and durability of the adaptive layer over the tire's wear life.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/09/nokian-tyres-celebrates-90th-anniversary-of-hakkapeliitta-tyres)

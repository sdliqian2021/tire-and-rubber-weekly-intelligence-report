### Nokian launches Hakkapeliitta 01 with temperature-responsive studs

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-10 | Nokian Tyres*

**What changed:** Nokian introduced an adaptive base layer that firms in ice and snow to push studs outward and softens on warmer pavement so they retract deeper into the tread.

**Why it matters:** Nokian claims up to 30% less road wear, 1 dB less cabin noise, 10% better ice grip and 5% better wet grip versus Hakkapeliitta 10; protocols were not supplied.

**Intelligence signal:** Mechanical winter grip is becoming condition-responsive at the material-structure interface rather than fixed for all surfaces.

**Sources:** [Tyrepress](https://www.tyrepress.com/2026/09/nokian-tyres-celebrates-90th-anniversary-of-hakkapeliitta-tyres)

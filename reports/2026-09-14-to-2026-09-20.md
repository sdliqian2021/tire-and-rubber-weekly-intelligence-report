### Goodyear updates UltraGrip Arctic 2 ahead of stricter studded-tire rules

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-14 | Goodyear*

**What changed:** Goodyear introduced an updated UltraGrip Arctic 2 with revised stud-pin geometry and the same stud count, positioning it for Finland's A+ road-wear requirement for new studded tires from 2027 and UNECE R164.

**Why it matters:** The design responds to a specific regulatory test regime, although the public performance evidence is based on the company's internal testing.

**Intelligence signal:** Nordic winter-tire development is shifting toward lower road wear without simply reducing stud count.

**Sources:** [Goodyear Europe Newsroom](https://news.goodyear.eu/sv-sv/utvecklat-foer-nordiska-vintrar-goodyear-uppdaterar-ultragrip-arctic-2)

### China carbon-black prices pull back after a feedstock-driven spike

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-19 | China carbon-black market*

**What changed:** Trade reports described tire-grade carbon-black prices near or above RMB10,000 per tonne before a Shandong N330 quotation of RMB11,300-11,500 per tonne fell by RMB350 on September 18-19; tire makers had issued price-increase letters as costs rose.

**Why it matters:** Carbon black is a major tire input, but the reported spot quotations and operating-rate estimates are market snapshots rather than audited transaction data.

**Intelligence signal:** A fast raw-material spike is passing through to tire pricing while showing early signs of reversal, increasing procurement and quotation risk.

### Michelin pilots NAZCA automated truck-tire regrooving in European workshops

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-14 | Michelin*

**What changed:** Michelin described about 20 NAZCA machines in European pilot use for more than a year; the system uses high-density laser measurement and adaptive control to set regrooving depth, with commercial rollout planned for 2027.

**Why it matters:** Multi-site pilot use provides industrial evidence, but the claimed 30-50% capacity gain and under-three-minute hands-on time are company-reported without an external baseline.

**Intelligence signal:** Automation is moving into tire-life-extension services, where workshop consistency and technician time constrain adoption.

**Sources:** [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/michelin-is-modernizing-regrooving-with-nazca)

### Xiaomi selects ZC Rubber ARISUN 1 Star for 19-inch N70 variants

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-15 | ZC Rubber*

**What changed:** ZC Rubber said its 245/55R19 ARISUN 1 Star is original equipment on all 19-inch Xiaomi SkyNomad N70 Pro and Max configurations; an independent trade publication corroborated the fitment.

**Why it matters:** A specified vehicle and size provide concrete customer evidence, while rolling-resistance, noise and grip claims remain supplier statements.

**Intelligence signal:** Chinese tire makers are converting EV-focused product development into named domestic premium-vehicle fitments.

**Sources:** [ZC Rubber](https://www.zc-rubber.com/index.php/news/news_info/27/349) | [second source](https://www.tiretechnologyinternational.com/news/oe-fitments/xiaomi-n70-chooses-zc-rubber-arisun-1-star-as-oe-tire.html)

### Hankook and Schmitz Cargobull begin four-year trailer-tire OE agreement

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--services">Sales &amp; Customer Services</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-17 | Hankook Tire*

**What changed:** Hankook said an original-equipment agreement with trailer maker Schmitz Cargobull took effect on July 1 and runs through June 2030, covering Smart Line, Smart Flex and Smart Work trailer products.

**Why it matters:** The named customer, product families and contract term provide concrete demand evidence, but public volume and pricing are absent.

**Intelligence signal:** Commercial-tire suppliers are converting regional product portfolios into multi-year trailer OE contracts.

**Sources:** [Hankook Tire Media Center](https://www.hankooktire-mediacenter.com/fr/communique-de-presse/news/hankook-et-schmitz-cargobull-annoncent-un-partenariat-pour-la-premiere-monte) | [second source](https://www.hankooktire-mediacenter.com/de/pressemappen/the-tire-cologne-2024/pressemitteilung/hankook-und-schmitz-cargobull-vereinbaren-erstausruestungs-partnerschaft-1)

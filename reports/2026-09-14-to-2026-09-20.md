## R&D & Product Development

### Goodyear updates UltraGrip Arctic 2 ahead of stricter studded-tire rules

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-14 | Goodyear*

Goodyear introduced an updated UltraGrip Arctic 2 that uses revised stud-pin geometry while retaining the existing stud count, with the design intended to meet Finland's A+ road-wear requirement for new studded tires from 2027 as well as UNECE R164. The notable change is a compliance-led redesign of the stud itself rather than a reduction in stud density, showing how winter-tire makers are adapting product architecture to tighter road-wear rules while seeking to preserve ice performance. This matters because similar regulation could influence development priorities, tooling and portfolio renewal across the Nordic studded-tire market. The next point to watch is independent validation of road wear and winter performance as the 2027 requirement approaches, since the evidence disclosed so far comes from Goodyear's internal testing.

**Sources:** [Goodyear Europe Newsroom](https://news.goodyear.eu/sv-sv/utvecklat-foer-nordiska-vintrar-goodyear-uppdaterar-ultragrip-arctic-2)

### Continental targets more than 650 UHP sizes by end-2027

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-16 | Continental*

Continental plans to expand its ultra-high-performance tire portfolio to more than 650 sizes by the end of 2027, after UHP products rose from 49% of its passenger-tire sales in 2020 to 62% in 2025. The notable development is the planned breadth of the range, which signals that larger rims and higher performance specifications are becoming mainstream rather than niche within Continental's passenger-tire business. For the tire industry, this shift can raise development, manufacturing and inventory complexity while increasing competition for premium fitments and replacement demand. The key milestone is delivery of the 650-size portfolio by end-2027, because the figure remains a target rather than a completed rollout.

**Sources:** [Continental](https://www.continental.com/en/press/press-releases/ultra-high-performance-tires)

*Published 2026-09-15 | Shandong Lianke Technology*

Shandong Lianke Technology approved an approximately RMB1 billion materials base in Egypt's Suez Canal Economic Zone, planned to produce 100,000 tonnes each of silica and carbon black annually on an 18-month construction schedule. *Published 2026-09-19 | China carbon-black market*

China's tire-grade carbon-black market showed sharp short-term volatility, with reported prices near or above RMB10,000 per tonne and a Shandong N330 quotation of RMB11,300-11,500 before falling RMB350 on September 18-19; tire makers also reportedly issued price-increase letters as input costs rose. Cheeshine introduced EP365, a plasticizer developed for high-silica tire compounds, at RubberTech China and reported lower Mooney viscosity, faster mixing and a 14-18% improvement in wear in company testing. If the claims hold in production, it could help tire makers improve mixing efficiency and compound processability while retaining wear performance, supporting broader use of silica-rich tread formulations. ### USW asks USTR to open a Section 201 tire investigation

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--marketing">Marketing &amp; Distribution</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-14 | United Steelworkers*

The United Steelworkers asked the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative to initiate a Section 201 safeguard investigation into tire imports, citing a 40% decline in domestic output since 2000, nine announced plant closures over two years and more than 7,000 affected jobs. The notable development is the choice of a broad safeguard mechanism focused on import injury, which could reach beyond the country-specific trade cases commonly used in the tire sector. Any investigation or remedy could materially change U.S. tire pricing, sourcing, import flows and domestic investment decisions. The key point to watch is whether USTR opens a case and, if so, how it defines product scope and injury, because no investigation or remedy had been initiated at publication.

**Sources:** [United Steelworkers](https://usw.org/press-release/usw-urges-ustr-to-launch-201-tire-investigation)

### China tire export volume rises through August while export value falls

*Published 2026-09-20 | China tire industry*

China exported 6.63 million tonnes of tires from January through August, up 2.0% year on year, while export value fell 3.1% to RMB110.639 billion; in August alone, volume declined 0.7% and value fell 4.1%. The notable divergence between rising year-to-date tonnage and lower value points to pressure from pricing, product mix or destination mix rather than straightforward export growth. This matters because weaker value realization can compress manufacturer and channel margins while intensifying competition in overseas replacement markets. Destination- and product-level data, as well as the direction of export unit values in the remaining months, will be important to watch because the current aggregates do not provide like-for-like pricing.

### China's rising NEV mix contrasts with weaker passenger-tire OE demand

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--marketing">Marketing &amp; Distribution</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-16 | China automotive and tire market*

China's new-energy vehicle sales reached 10.65 million in January-August, up 10.7% year on year, while a China Tire Business analysis estimated passenger-tire OE demand at 86.67 million units, down 5.35%, and commercial-tire OE demand up 7.45%. The notable contrast is that rapid NEV growth is not translating into broad passenger-tire OE growth, highlighting the importance of total vehicle output, segment mix and fitment assumptions rather than NEV penetration alone. Tire suppliers therefore need segment-specific capacity and account planning instead of treating electrification as a uniform demand tailwind. Official tire-shipment data and second-half vehicle production will be the main checks on the analysis, since the tire-demand figures are estimates rather than reported shipments.

**Sources:** [China Tire Business](https://www.tirechina.net/833072.html)

Prinx introduced its ELD12 earthmover tire and plans to roll it out more broadly in Europe through its dealer network.

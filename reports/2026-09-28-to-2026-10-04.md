### Bridgestone unveils two-tire Potenza system for Formula E GEN4

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-10-01 | Bridgestone and Formula E*

Bridgestone introduced an all-weather Potenza GEN4 and separate heavy-wet Typhoon tire as Formula E sole supplier for 2026/27; development included more than 6,500 km of testing. Electric racing is returning to condition-specific tires while using recovered carbon black and rice-husk silica. GEN4 creates a demanding laboratory, but race durability and lifecycle comparisons are not public. Watch homologation, race degradation and transfer to road tires.

**Sources:** [Bridgestone global newsroom and technical feature](https://www.bridgestone.com/corporate/news/2026100101.html) | [second source](https://fe.bridgestone.com/feature/tech/sog1)

### POLYN ships engineering nodes for in-tire road-grip estimation

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-09-28 | POLYN Technology*

POLYN began delivering VibroSense TMS engineering chips for sensor nodes that estimate peak friction coefficient from accelerometer signals at the edge. Tire sensing is moving from pressure and temperature toward local inference of road grip. Customer evaluation is tangible, but the braking claim lacks a published protocol and repeat statistics. Watch named evaluators, test protocol, qualification and production tape-out.

**Sources:** [POLYN Technology newsroom](https://polyn.ai/polyn-delivers-first-vibrosense-tms-engineering-sensor-nodes-for-customer-evaluation) | [second source](https://polyn.ai/vibrosense-tms-now-in-silicon)

### Continental launches regional truck tires with estimated 19-22% mileage gains

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-10-01 | Continental*

Continental launched HSR 5 EP steer and HDR 5 EP drive tires, claiming estimated removal-mileage gains of 22% and 19% versus predecessors while maintaining low rolling resistance. Truck-tire development is optimizing tread life, fuel efficiency and casing retreadability as one cost system. The estimates could lower cost per mile, but test method and conditions are undisclosed. Watch fleet trials, rolling-resistance data and realized removal mileage.

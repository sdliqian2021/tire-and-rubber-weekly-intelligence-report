### Pirelli debuts a new Moto2 soft rear structure for Mandalika heat

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span></p>

*Published 2026-10-06 | Pirelli*

Pirelli introduced F0515, a soft Moto2 rear using the existing SC0 compound with a revised structure intended to improve stability and performance consistency at Mandalika, where track temperatures can exceed 60 C. Race-tire development is separating compound choice from structural tuning to target circuit load and heat. Watch race usage, wear and whether the structure transfers to other circuits or production products.

### US Commerce preliminarily sets a 107.57% countervailing rate on Chinese CBS accelerator

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--materials">Materials &amp; Supply Chain</span> <span class="story-origin story-origin--china" title="China-related story">China</span></p>

*Published 2026-10-06 | US Department of Commerce*

Commerce announced an affirmative preliminary countervailing-duty determination covering N-cyclohexylbenzothiazole-2-sulfenamide from China, listing 107.57% for named respondents and all other producers or exporters. Trade controls are reaching a tire-compounding accelerator rather than only finished tires or base polymers. A high preliminary rate can alter sourcing and landed cost, but it is not the final duty order. Watch the final determination, possible alignment with the trade commission and supplier sourcing changes.

**Sources:** [International Trade Administration](https://www.trade.gov/preliminary-affirmative-countervailing-duty-determination-n-cyclohexylbenzothiazole-2-sulfenamide)

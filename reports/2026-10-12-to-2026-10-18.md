*Published 2026-10-12 | Bridgestone and Lamborghini*

Bridgestone China's release introduced the POTENZA Race R custom fitment for the 2026 Lamborghini Revuelto SV in 20-inch front and 21-inch rear sizes, developed with virtual tire development and a motorsport-derived compound. China-facing product communication is carrying quantified virtual-development claims into the premium OE segment. Watch independent track results, production volumes and further OE applications.

### Continental details concept tire behind Volkswagen Mission Efficiency record

<p class="story-labels" aria-label="Story classification"><span class="story-category story-category--rd">R&amp;D &amp; Product Development</span> <span class="story-signal story-signal--repeat" title="Recap or repeated signal">Recap / repeat</span></p>

*Published 2026-10-15 | Continental and Volkswagen*

Continental disclosed the tire contribution to Volkswagen's certified 1,278.36 km Wolfsburg-to-Vienna efficiency run. The vehicle used 7.51 kWh/100 km including charging losses, while tire rolling resistance was reported at 4.9 kg/t, about 25% below the EU class A threshold. Tire architecture is being optimized as a quantified part of whole-vehicle electric-efficiency demonstrations. The record is a system result and cannot assign all energy performance to the tire; the run occurred before the weekly publication. Watch production feasibility, wear and independent repeat tests.

**Sources:** [Continental](https://www.continental.com/en/press/press-releases/volkswagen-concept-tire-world-record)
